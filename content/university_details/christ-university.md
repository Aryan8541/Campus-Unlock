## 2. About

CHRIST (Deemed to be University) was founded in 1969 as Christ College and was recognized as a Deemed to be University in 2008 by what was then the Ministry of Human Resource Development, with UGC approval. It runs campuses in Bengaluru, Pune (Lavasa), and Delhi NCR, and is described as attracting students from over 77 countries across its on-campus programs. Its online arm, CHRIST Online Programmes, is presented as the official digital platform for degree-seeking distance learners, separate from short online certificate/diploma offerings that may sit on a different sub-site.

**Flag:** Two distinct-looking properties were seen in search results — `online.christuniversity.in` and `onlineprogrammes.christuniversity.in`. ## 11. Learning Methodology

- Academic mentors assigned to online students
- LMS with self-learning material repository plus e-content
- Live sessions and doubt-clearing sessions with faculty (i.e., not a purely asynchronous/self-paced model — some synchronous component exists)
- Dedicated support team for academic, technical, and administrative queries (email, call, live chat per official FAQ)
- Exams require a desktop/laptop with internet, browser, and webcam — implying proctored online exams
- Certificates typically issued within 60 days of program completion (per official FAQ)
- Online certificates/diplomas are stated to hold equal value/recognition to on-campus equivalents, per UGC guidelines (official framing — standard boilerplate language most universities use, include but don't over-weight as differentiation)

## 12. FAQs (draft, sourced from official FAQ page)

- **Can I apply for a CHRIST online degree?** Yes, if eligibility criteria are met — Indian citizens only at present.
- **Do online certificates/diplomas carry the same value as on-campus ones?** Yes, per UGC guidelines, as stated on the official site.
- **What do I need for exams?** A desktop or laptop with internet access, a browser, and a webcam.
- **How is student support handled?** A dedicated team is available via email, call, and live chat, with hours listed on the student dashboard.
