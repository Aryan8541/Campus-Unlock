## ⚠️ Read this before anything else

**Unlike IIT Madras and IIT Kanpur (both profiled elsewhere in this project, both of which run genuine full online degree programs), I could not find a full online degree program at IIT Kharagpur.** One independent aggregator states this directly and explicitly: *"No, IIT Kharagpur doesn't provide an Online M.Tech for working professionals."* IIT Kharagpur's own official admissions page describes only standard on-campus postgraduate routes — Joint M.Tech/MCP-PhD (via GATE), MBA (full-time), Integrated M.Sc, and Joint M.Sc-PhD (via JAM) — with no online-mode equivalents mentioned.

What does exist, per a general-purpose aggregator (not IIT Kharagpur's own site): "minor courses that can be completed online mode in a short duration" and "micro-specialisation courses," offered alongside the regular on-campus programs. These sound more like short online modules/electives than a standalone degree program, similar in spirit to IIT Delhi and IIT Bombay's diploma/certificate offerings — but even less well-defined in the sources found, since no specific program name, fee, or duration could be confirmed for these "minor" or "micro-specialisation" courses.

**This page is closer in spirit to the IIT Delhi/Bombay situation than to IIT Madras/Kanpur — recommend treating it the same way: don't position it as an "online degree" comparison entry without further direct confirmation from IIT Kharagpur of what, specifically, is currently on offer online.**

## About

IIT Kharagpur, founded on 18 August 1951, was the first of the IITs to be established. It offers a broad range of on-campus undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs — including 15+ B.Tech programs, and postgraduate routes leading to Joint M.Tech/MCP-PhD, MBA, and MMST degrees, plus Integrated M.Sc and Joint M.Sc-PhD programs. Admission to postgraduate programs is via GATE (M.Tech) or JAM (M.Sc), consistent with standard IIT admission practice.
