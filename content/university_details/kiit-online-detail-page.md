## About

Kalinga Institute of Industrial Technology (KIIT) was founded in 1992 by Dr. Achyuta Samanta, beginning as an Industrial Training Institute with just twelve students and two faculty members. It was conferred deemed-to-be university status in 2004 and renamed "KIIT University" — before being renamed back to "Kalinga Institute of Industrial Technology" (KIIT) in 2017, retaining its deemed-university status throughout (see flags on this naming history). Its Office of the Director, Distance and Online Education runs KIIT Online, offering UGC-DEB approved online undergraduate and postgraduate degrees.

## Programs, Fees & Eligibility
