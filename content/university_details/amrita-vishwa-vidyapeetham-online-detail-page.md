## About

Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham Online operates under the brand **Amrita AHEAD** (Amrita Hub for Excellence in Academics and Development) — the dedicated online education division of Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, a deemed-to-be university headquartered in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. The parent university was founded in 1994 by Mata Amritanandamayi Devi (Amma) with the opening of Amrita School of Engineering, Coimbatore, and was granted deemed university status by UGC in 2003. It operates across 10+ campuses in Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, and Haryana, with international centers in the USA and UK.

Amrita AHEAD launched its UGC-approved online degree programs in 2021, making it one of the earlier entrants among top-ranked Indian universities to offer fully online degrees. The online division offers 6 core degree programs — BBA, BCA, B.Com at UG level and MBA, MCA, M.Com at PG level — along with certificate programs. All programs are UGC-DEB approved and equivalent to the parent university's on-campus degrees. The online portal is at **onlineamrita.com**; admissions run quarterly (January, April, July, October sessions).

## Admission Process

Confirmed via official amrita.edu launch press release and multiple aggregators:

1. Register on the official online portal: **onlineamrita.com**
2. Fill in personal information (name, DOB, gender, contact)
3. Complete application form — select program and specialization, fill academic details
4. Pay application fee of ₹500 online (debit/credit card, net banking)
5. Upload required documents
6. Pay program fee to confirm admission (installment options available)

**No entrance exam required** — admission is merit-based on prior qualifying examination marks. Confirmed across 4+ sources.

**Admission cycles:** January, April, July, October (quarterly) — confirmed by EduFolio and multiple aggregators. **July 2026 last date:** 27 July 2026 (per AdmissionDIY, July 2026 session).
