## About

IIT (BHU) Varanasi traces its roots to the erstwhile Institute of Technology, Banaras Hindu University (IT-BHU), and was converted to full Indian Institute of Technology status by an Act of Parliament on 29 June 2012. As an Institute of National Importance, it offers BTech, BArch, MTech, MPharm, and PhD programs across Ceramic, Chemical, Computer Science, Civil, Systems, Electrical, Electronics, Industrial Management, Mechanical Engineering, and Humanities & Social Sciences, admitting students through JEE Advanced (BTech/BArch), CUET-PG/GATE (postgraduate), and written test/interview (PhD). Its individual online course offerings, as catalogued by Class Central, are **MOOC-style, single-course, non-degree content** — spanning technical topics (mathematical optimization for machine learning, multiphase flow fundamentals) and, notably, even non-technical wellness-oriented content (a course on using poetry and literature to build resilience against anxiety and trauma) — a genuinely eclectic mix consistent with NPTEL/SWAYAM-style open-course content rather than a structured degree curriculum.

**Name-confusion note (high priority, connects to earlier research in this series):** IIT (BHU) Varanasi shares its physical campus and the "BHU" name element with the separate **Banaras Hindu University**, a central university profiled independently in an earlier batch of this research series, where a genuine (if thinly-documented) "BHU Online" degree offering was described. **This current research pass directly surfaced evidence of the conflation risk already anticipated in that earlier profile**: one aggregator source's "BHU online courses" listing (38 items) appears, based on its content description (NIRF Engineering-category rank, QS World University Ranking mention, entrance exams like JEE Advanced/NATA/IIT JAM), to actually describe **IIT (BHU)**, not the broader Banaras Hindu University — meaning this specific source should **not** be used as evidence for the separately-profiled "BHU Online" degree listing, and any data already drawn from it for that profile should be re-checked.
