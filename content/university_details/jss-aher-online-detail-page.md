## About

JSS Academy of Higher Education & Research (JSS AHER), formerly JSS University, is a deemed-to-be university established in 2008 under JSS Mahavidyapeetha, with its main campus in Mysuru. Its Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE) — also referred to on its own site as the "Online Education Programme" (OEP) — offers UGC-entitled online undergraduate and postgraduate degrees, distinct from the university's separate on-campus and open & distance learning (ODL) offerings.

## Highlights

- NAAC re-accredited to **A++ (3.61 CGPA) in October 2024**, up from A+ (3.47–3.48 CGPA, 2018 cycle) — a genuine, well-documented upgrade, confirmed both by a dedicated JSSMC newsroom announcement and by current third-party sources
- UGC Category I Deemed University status
- Recognized by the Ministry of Education (formerly MHRD)
- Certified for e-learning excellence by **QS I-GAUGE E-LEAD**, with a stated score of 150/150 for academic digitalization and online-education readiness — a distinctive credential not seen in the other universities profiled so far
- Part of JSS Mahavidyapeetha, a large multi-institution trust with a strong healthcare, pharmacy, and medical-sciences footprint
- **Programs are niche and industry-specific** (hospital administration, healthcare management, pharmacy administration) rather than a broad general catalog — see flags
