## About

Yashwantrao Chavan Maharashtra Open University (YCMOU) was established on **1 July 1989** in Nashik, named after Yashwantrao Chavan, Maharashtra's first Chief Minister, and incorporated under Act XX (1989) of the Maharashtra State Legislature. Reported by one source as the **fifth-largest open university in India**, YCMOU is recognized under Section 12(B) of the UGC Act and follows a mission to "reach the unreached" — extending flexible Open and Distance Learning (ODL) access especially to underserved and rural populations across Maharashtra. It offers 120+ programmes spanning certificate, diploma, UG, PG, and PhD levels across arts, commerce, science, agriculture, journalism, computer applications, and other disciplines, and has won the **Commonwealth of Learning Institutional Excellence Award twice (2002 and 2019)** — a genuine, specific, internationally-sourced recognition not seen for most other universities in this research series.
