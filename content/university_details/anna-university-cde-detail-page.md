## About

Anna University was founded in 1978 in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, as a public state technical university, and is well known for its engineering, science, arts, humanities, and management programs. Its distance/online education wing, the **Centre for Distance Education (CDE)** — with an "Online Cell" specifically handling its online-mode programs — was established, per most sources, in **2007**. Unlike several other universities profiled across this research series, Anna University's distance/online catalogue is **postgraduate-only**: one source explicitly states "Anna University does not offer UG courses through its distant education mode." The Centre offers professional MBA, MCA, and M.Sc. programs, with 11 specialization options across these, aimed at building "technology and managerial competencies" for working professionals.

## Highlights

- Two distinctly online-badged MBA programs — **MBA (Business Analytics)** and **MBA (General Management)** — are explicitly stated on the official CDE website to be **"UGC-DEB approved and entitled"**, a specific, direct, primary-source confirmation
- **Postgraduate-only distance/online catalogue** — no UG courses offered through distance mode, per one source's explicit statement; this is a notable structural difference from most other universities in this research series, which typically offer both UG and PG distance programs
- More than 10 study centres in and outside Chennai, per one source
- Entrance-test-based admission for MBA and MCA specifically — **Distance Education Entrance Test (DEET)** or **TANCET (Tamil Nadu Common Entrance Test)** — a mandatory step, not an exception, unlike several other universities in this series where entrance tests applied only to specific programs within an otherwise merit-based system

## Admission Process

1. Visit the official CDE/Online Cell portal (onlinecde.annauniv.edu or cde.annauniv.edu — see flag above about these potentially being distinct portals)
2. Apply online with a recognized bachelor's degree, minimum 50% aggregate marks
3. Clear the required entrance test — **OEET** for the online-badged MBA programs, or **DEET/TANCET** for the "Distance Education" track and for MCA, per the sources reviewed
4. Submit required documents (SSLC/10th mark sheet, HSC/12th mark sheet, and further documents per program)
5. Pay the application fee — reported as **₹300** by one source and **₹500** (via Demand Draft, payable to "The Director, Centre for Distance Education, Anna University") by another — **these two figures conflict and should be reconciled**, possibly reflecting different years or different specific programs
6. Complete enrollment; interactive live classes with quizzes, digital resources, and recorded sessions follow, per one source
