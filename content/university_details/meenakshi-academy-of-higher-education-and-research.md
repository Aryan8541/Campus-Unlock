## 2. About

MAHER was established in 2004 and is recognized as a Deemed to be University by the UGC under Section 3 of the UGC Act, 1956. It runs several constituent institutions across Chennai and Kanchipuram, including Meenakshi Medical College Hospital & Research Institute, Meenakshi Ammal Dental College, Meenakshi Engineering College, and Meenakshi College of Nursing. Distance/online learners are served through a separate Institute of Distance Education (IDE), founded in 2011, and a Centre for Online Education for the fully online catalogue.

**Flag:** Sources vary on whether "MAHER Distance Education" and "MAHER Online" are the same administrative unit or two separate ones (IDE vs. Centre for Online Education). Treat as two related but distinct program tracks until confirmed on maher.ac.in.
