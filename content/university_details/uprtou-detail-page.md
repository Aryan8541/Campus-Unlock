## About

Uttar Pradesh Rajarshi Tandon Open University (UPRTOU) is a state-run public open university established in 1999, named after freedom fighter Rajarshi Purushottam Das Tandon. Headquartered in Prayagraj, it delivers programs through both **Open and Distance Learning (ODL)** and a separately designated **Online Learning (OL)** mode — the university's own site explicitly distinguishes between the two (for example, clarifying that its Yoga programs are restricted to the ONLINE mode only, while a separate Yoga offering runs under ODL) — so these should not be treated as interchangeable when describing a specific program (see flags).

## Highlights

- NAAC accredited **B+ Grade**, per the official 3 January 2023 accreditation cycle — consistent across the official site and every aggregator checked, no conflicting grade found
- Ranked **No. 3 nationally** in NIRF's newly introduced **"Open Universities"** category for **2025** — again, consistent across multiple independent sources, a genuinely clean ranking claim
- UGC-recognised under Section 2(f) of the UGC Act, 1956; also recognised by the Distance Education Council (DEC), NCTE (for teacher-education programs), AICTE, and UGC's Distance Education Bureau (DEB)

- Study centers spread across multiple UP cities: Gorakhpur, Lucknow, Bareilly, Prayagraj, Varanasi, Ghaziabad, Meerut, Agra, Jhansi, Kanpur, Ayodhya, and Azamgarh

## Admission Process

1. Visit the official UPRTOU admission portal (uprtouadm.samarth.edu.in, a Samarth-eGov-based portal)
2. Register and complete the online application form
3. **Create the mandatory ABC ID and DEB ID** (a UGC-driven requirement, same pattern seen at other universities profiled)
4. Upload required documents and pay the applicable fee
5. For B.Ed/B.Ed (Special Education): sit the university's own entrance exam; other programs are merit-based

Admission runs twice yearly (January and July cycles). ⚠️ Exact deadline dates found across sources conflict and are cycle-dependent (one source cites a 10 July 2026 submission deadline for the 2026-27 session with a 15 March 2026 application-window start; another references an "expected 5 June 2026" start for the same broad cycle) — treat as approximate and reconfirm against the live official notice before publishing a specific date.
