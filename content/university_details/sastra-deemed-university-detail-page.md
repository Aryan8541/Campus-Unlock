## About

SASTRA (Shanmugha Arts, Science, Technology & Research Academy), commonly known as SASTRA Deemed University, was founded in 1984 in Thanjavur, Tamil Nadu, and conferred Deemed-to-be-University status in 2001. It is described as the first deemed university in Tamil Nadu by one source. SASTRA offers undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs on campus across engineering, management, law, sciences, humanities, and education, and has extended select programs into a fully online mode, delivered in partnership with TCS iON's learning and placement platform.

## Highlights

- UGC-DEB approved for its online programs — confirmed on the official SASTRA Online domain (sastraonline.com)
- NAAC A++ accredited (majority of sources — see flags for one outlier)
- Ranked among the top 25–30 Indian universities by NIRF (exact figure varies by year/source — see Rankings)
- Online programs delivered in partnership with TCS iON, giving online students access to a placement portal
- Structured per NEP 2020's Multiple Exit Options for UG online programs (1-year certificate / 2-year diploma / 3-year degree)
- AICTE approved (confirmed specifically for the MBA program)

## Admission Process

1. Apply directly through the SASTRA Online portal (sastraonline.com)
2. Direct-entry framework — applicants submit academic records digitally

4. Document verification and fee payment
5. LMS access and TCS iON platform onboarding
