## About

Chitkara University's roots trace to the Chitkara Educational Trust (CET), founded in 1997 by Dr. Ashok K. Chitkara and Madhu Chitkara, which established the Chitkara Institute of Engineering & Technology in 2002. The university itself was formally constituted through "The Chitkara University Act" by the Punjab State Legislature — most sources date this to **2010**, though one source states 2008. Its online wing, Chitkara University Online, offers UGC-DEB and AICTE approved undergraduate and postgraduate programs across business management, computer science, engineering, nursing, medical laboratory technology, architecture, and hotel management.

**Flag:** Three different "establishment" years appear across sources for different milestones — 1997 (educational trust founded), 2002 (engineering institute founded), 2008 or 2010 (university legally constituted). These describe genuinely different events in the institution's history and should not be collapsed into a single "established" date on the profile — recommend using 2010 (university-act year, appearing in two sources) as the primary "established" field, with the trust's 1997 origin noted separately in the About section.
