## About

Banaras Hindu University is a central university in Varanasi, popularly described as Asia's largest residential university, spread across a 1,300-acre campus with 41 boys' hostels and 21 girls' hostels. It has been designated an "Institute of Eminence" by the Government of India in recognition of its academic contribution. Alongside its large on-campus programs, BHU has begun offering select online degree programs — described by one source as "BHU Online" — delivered through a Learning Management System (LMS), extending access to learners beyond the Varanasi campus.

**Flag:** Unlike the well-documented, long-established distance wings seen at universities like Pondicherry or Jamia Millia Islamia (both with named directorates/centres founded decades ago), sources in this pass did not clearly name a specific administrative unit (e.g., "Directorate of Distance Education" or "Centre for Online Education") for BHU's online offering — it's referred to generically as "BHU Online" across sources. This should be confirmed against BHU's official domain (bhu.ac.in) before publishing, to identify the actual administrative body responsible and its official name.
