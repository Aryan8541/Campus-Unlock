## About

Indira Gandhi National Open University (IGNOU) was established in 1985 under an Act of Parliament and operates under the Ministry of Education, Government of India. Starting with just two programmes and around 4,500 students, it has grown into what multiple sources describe as the largest university in the world by enrolment, serving over 4 million learners through 21 Schools of Studies and a network of regional centres (cited as 56–67 depending on the source — see flags) plus 29 overseas partner institutions. IGNOU delivers programmes through both **Open and Distance Learning (ODL)** and **Online Learning (OL)** modes.

## Highlights

- NAAC accredited **A++ Grade for the 2021–26 cycle** — IGNOU's own site states it was the **first Open University in the country** to receive NAAC's highest grade; this is confirmed consistently across every official and third-party source checked, with no conflicting grade found anywhere
- Ranked **No. 1 nationally** in NIRF's **"Open University" category** for **both 2024 and 2025** — again, unusually clean agreement across all sources, official and aggregator alike
- ⚠️ **Regulatory nuance, not a compliance gap:** per IGNOU's own official Common Prospectus, the university has been formally **exempted** from the applicability of the UGC (Open and Distance Learning Programme and Online Programme) Regulations, via specific UGC letters dated 9 August 2019 (DEB-I) and 17 March 2020 (OL) — meaning IGNOU does not carry a standard "UGC-DEB approved" badge the way other universities in this batch do, not because it lacks recognition, but because it predates and sits outside that specific regulatory framework by explicit UGC exemption
- No. 1 rank in the CFIs (Non-Technical) category of the Atal Ranking of Institutions on Innovation Achievement (ARIIA), 2021; a Five-Star MoE rating for innovation/startup support (2020); and 2nd-cleanest among non-residential university HEIs (UGC category, 2019) — per IGNOU's own rankings page
- 200+ to 300+ programmes across Bachelor's, Master's, Diploma, Certificate, and Doctoral levels (see flags on the exact current count)

## Admission Process

1. Register on the official IGNOU admission portal
2. **Create the mandatory DEB ID** (per UGC guidelines, required once per student for ODL/online enrolment — same pattern as UPRTOU and KSOU)
3. Complete the online application form and upload required documents
4. Pay the applicable program fee
5. Admissions open **twice yearly**, in **January and July** cycles; examinations (Term-End Exams) are conducted twice yearly, in **June and December**
