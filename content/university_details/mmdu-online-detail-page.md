## About

Maharishi Markandeshwar (Deemed to be University), commonly known as MM(DU) or MMU, was established in 1993 in Mullana, Haryana, and has grown from an institute offering graduate-level education into a large multi-disciplinary deemed university. ⚠️ **This is a completely different institution from "Maharshi Dayanand University" (MDU), Rohtak** — a similarly-named but entirely separate state university in Haryana; the two are easy to confuse and shouldn't be conflated (see flags). MM(DU)'s Centre for Distance and Online Education manages the university's online and distance-learning operations, offering UGC-DEB approved undergraduate and postgraduate degrees.
