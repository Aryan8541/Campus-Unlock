## About

Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Gorakhpur University (informally "Gorakhpur University") was the first university to emerge as a leading higher-education centre in Uttar Pradesh after Indian independence. Established by a UP legislative act in 1956 and functionally operational from 1 September 1957, it now holds one of the strongest NAAC accreditation records of any state university in India. Its Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE) offers UGC-entitled online undergraduate and postgraduate programs at notably affordable fees, consistent with its status as a government-funded institution.
