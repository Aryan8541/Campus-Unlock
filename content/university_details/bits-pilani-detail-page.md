## ⚠️ Important framing note before anything else

BITS Pilani's online offering is fundamentally different in structure from the other universities profiled on this platform, and this should shape how it's presented:

- **WILP (the large majority of BITS's online catalog — 50+ PG programs) requires the applicant to already be a working professional**, typically with a minimum of 1 year of relevant work experience (2+ years for some specializations), plus mandatory employer and mentor consent forms. It is not an open-enrollment "anyone can join" online degree in the way most other universities on this platform are. Prospective students who aren't currently employed in a relevant field would not be eligible for most WILP programs.
- Separately, BITS Pilani also offers **at least one genuinely open-enrollment online undergraduate degree** — a B.Sc. in Computer Science delivered via Coursera under BITS's Institution of Eminence status — which does not require work experience. This is a different, newer, and much smaller track than WILP.
- These two tracks should probably be described separately rather than blended into one "online programs" list, since they serve very different audiences and have different eligibility bars.

## About

BITS Pilani is a deemed-to-be university under Section 3 of the UGC Act, 1956, tracing its roots to 1901 as a school, becoming a full college by the 1950s-60s, and gaining university status in 1964. It was named one of India's first six "Institutions of Eminence" by the Ministry of Education in 2018. BITS launched its Work Integrated Learning Programmes (WILP) in 2001 to let working professionals pursue BITS degrees without leaving their jobs, and has since added a directly-online B.Sc. Computer Science degree (via Coursera) under its Institution of Eminence status, which — unlike WILP — doesn't require the applicant to already be employed.

## Admission Process

**WILP track:**
1. Apply online through the BITS Pilani WILP website
2. Confirm eligibility — including the work-experience requirement and, for many programs, employer/mentor consent
3. BITS WILP Admission Test (entrance test) — most programs
4. Personal interview (for some programs; candidates with qualifying national fellowships like CSIR-NET/JRF or GATE may be exempted from the written test but still need an interview)
5. Shortlisting based on merit

**Open-enrollment B.Sc. Computer Science track (different process):**
1. Apply directly via the Coursera-hosted BITS degree page
2. No work experience required
3. Offered twice yearly (February and August intake cycles, per the official page)
