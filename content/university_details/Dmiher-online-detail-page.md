## About

DMIHER (also known by its earlier name, DMIMS) is a deemed university located on a 125-acre campus in Sawangi-Meghe, Wardha, Maharashtra, primarily known for programs in Medicine, Dentistry, Nursing, Physiotherapy, Ayurveda, Pharmacy, and Allied Health Sciences. Its distance/online education is delivered through a dedicated Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE), which one source dates to 2019. The online/distance catalogue is narrower and distinct from the university's flagship medical programs, focused instead on management, commerce, computer applications, and healthcare-administration-adjacent postgraduate diplomas (e.g., Master of Hospital Administration, PG diplomas in medical/nursing education).

**Flag:** Sources give genuinely different "establishment" reference points: one source states the university was **"established in 1988 under the Bombay Trust Act"** before being granted deemed-university status in 2005, while most other sources simply cite **2005** as the founding year without mentioning an earlier 1988 trust-level origin. This mirrors the pattern seen with Chitkara University (trust vs. university-act founding dates) and should be resolved the same way — likely 2005 is the correct "established as a university" date, with any 1988 trust-level predecessor noted separately if it can be corroborated.
