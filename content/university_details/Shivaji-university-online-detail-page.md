## About

Shivaji University was established in 1962 in Kolhapur, Maharashtra, named after the Maratha warrior-king Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj, and inaugurated by Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan (then President of India) — consistent with Radhakrishnan's actual presidential term (1962–1967). Its Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE), formerly the Directorate of Distance Education (DDE), offers UGC-DEB approved distance and online undergraduate, postgraduate, and diploma programmes across Arts, Commerce, Science, and Management.

## Programs, Fees & Eligibility

**Fees — one specific, internally-consistent figure found:**
- **Distance MBA: approximately ₹20,530 per annum**, plus **₹1,950 registration fee** and **₹815 study centre fee** (per CampusOption)

Eligibility: a bachelor's degree from a recognized university for PG programmes (MBA, M.Com, M.Sc); Class 12 pass for UG programmes. Admission is largely merit-based.
