## About

Mangalayatan University is a private university established in 2006 in Beswan, near Aligarh, Uttar Pradesh (not to be confused with a separate Mangalayatan University campus operating in Jabalpur, Madhya Pradesh — a distinct sister institution; see flags). Its online/distance division delivers UGC-DEB approved undergraduate, postgraduate, and diploma programs across Arts, Commerce, Science, Management, Computer Applications, and Library Science, through a dedicated Learning Management System (LMS) with recorded and live-lecture delivery.
