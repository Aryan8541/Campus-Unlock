## ⚠️ Read this before anything else

**Same structural situation as IIT Delhi, profiled elsewhere in this project — worth repeating here rather than assuming it carries over:**

1. **IITs are not under UGC's jurisdiction.** Per UGC-DEB's own official FAQ page, IITs, IIMs, IISc, and other Institutes of National Importance fall outside UGC's purview. "UGC-DEB approved" is not the right framework to describe IIT Bombay's online offerings — it's not that they lack approval, it's that this specific approval category doesn't apply to them at all.
2. **What IIT Bombay offers online is a Diploma, not a full degree**, and this is even more explicit here than at IIT Delhi: the flagship program found — the **e-PG Diploma in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science**, run by IIT Bombay's Centre for Machine Intelligence and Data Science (C-MInDS) — is described by its own program materials as granting graduates **"IIT Bombay alumni status"** upon completion. Needing to explicitly grant alumni status as a program benefit is itself a signal that the diploma is not equivalent to holding a regular IIT Bombay degree, where alumni status would be automatic. IIT Bombay's actual degree programs (B.Tech, M.Tech, MBA, etc.) remain on-campus and entrance-exam based (JEE, GATE, CAT).
3. **One low-quality aggregator (CollegeVidya) uses loose language calling these "online degree programs,"** which conflicts with the more specific, authoritative sources (the program's own C-MInDS page, and independent program-review sites) that consistently and explicitly call it a Diploma. Going with "Diploma" as accurate and treating the "degree" framing as an aggregator's imprecise language, not a genuine alternative fact.

**Recommended framing:** same as IIT Delhi — present this as IIT Bombay's Online Diploma / MOOC offering, not as an "online degree" comparable to the UGC-DEB universities elsewhere in this project.

## About

IIT Bombay, founded in 1958, is one of India's leading technical institutes, with its regular degree admissions conducted through national entrance exams (JEE for B.Tech, GATE for M.Tech, CAT for MBA). Its online outreach runs through several channels: the **Centre for Distance Engineering Education Programme (C-DEEP)**, the **Centre for Machine Intelligence and Data Science (C-MInDS)** — which runs the flagship e-PG Diploma in AI & Data Science — the **IITBombayX** MOOC platform (hosted on edX), and NPTEL/SWAYAM. IIT Bombay also partners with third-party ed-tech platforms like Great Learning for some diploma delivery.

## Highlights

- e-PG Diploma in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science — an 18-month, faculty-delivered program via C-MInDS, aimed at early- and mid-career professionals
- Grants "IIT Bombay alumni status" and an in-person graduation ceremony on campus upon completion (per the program's own materials)
- IITBombayX offers MOOCs across four tracks: EduMOOCs, LifeMOOCs, SkillMOOCs, and TeachMOOCs
- C-DEEP handles large-scale distance outreach, including training up to 10,000 teachers at a time in blended mode
- Some diploma programs delivered in partnership with third-party ed-tech platforms (e.g., Great Learning)
