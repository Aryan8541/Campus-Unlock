The Court affirmed that Thapar (TIET) lacked the necessary UGC and AICTE approvals to award B.Tech degrees through distance learning, and that degrees awarded without those approvals are invalid. This is separate from — but consistent with — the broader Supreme Court/AICTE position that engineering degrees generally cannot be legally awarded through distance mode in India (a 2017 ruling, Orissa Lift Irrigation Corp. Ltd. v. Rabi Sankar Patro, suspended engineering degrees awarded via distance mode nationally for the 2001-2005 enrollment window, and AICTE has not approved distance B.Tech since).
