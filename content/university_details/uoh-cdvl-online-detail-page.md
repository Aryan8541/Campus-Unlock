## About

The University of Hyderabad is a leading central university focused primarily on postgraduate teaching and research, located in Gachibowli, Hyderabad. Its distance-education arm, the **Centre for Distance and Virtual Learning (CDVL)**, was established in **1994** and — per the university's own official CDVL website — has grown from offering just two diploma programs to its current slate of **14 one-year PG/Diploma programmes**, jointly approved by a UGC–DEB–AICTE joint committee. More than 30,000 participants have been admitted across CDVL's history, per the same official source. CDVL also jointly offers diploma programmes in partnership with external bodies including NIRD&PR (National Institute of Rural Development & Panchayati Raj), Truth Labs, Apollo Med Skills, IFCAI (Infection Control Academy of India), the LV Prasad Eye Institute, and 360DigiTMG.

## Admission Process

1. Create the mandatory ABC ID and DEB ID per latest UGC guidelines (confirmed by an aggregator source, consistent with the general UGC-wide requirement seen for several other universities in this series)
2. Applications processed through the **Samarth portal** (per the official CDVL source) — note this differs from the generic "visit the university's website" flow described by most aggregators, and is a more specific, checkable detail
3. Admission cycles run twice yearly: July/August and January/February (per multiple sources)
4. Complete the application form with self-attested copies of relevant certificates
5. Pay the registration fee (₹300, confirmed) and student fees, collected semester-wise through the UoH SBI portal, per the official source
6. Selection communicated to candidates via email
