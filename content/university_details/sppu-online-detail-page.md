## Admission Process

1. Visit the official SPPU / School of Open Learning website (reported as http://unipune.ac.in/sol/)
2. Find the admission/apply section and register with name, email, and phone number
3. Log in with newly created credentials and complete the online application form
4. For most UG and PG programs: merit-based selection using Class 12 or bachelor's degree marks (no entrance test)
5. **Exception — MBA:** requires a Common Entrance Test (CET) score; one older source also mentions SPPU's own Online Entrance Examination (OEE), JEE, or CUET as accepted routes depending on program — **this detail is inconsistent across sources and should be confirmed per-program, since MBA admission clearly does not follow the general merit-only rule**
6. Submit required documents and pay the application fee
