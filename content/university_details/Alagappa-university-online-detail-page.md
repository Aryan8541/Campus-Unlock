## About

Alagappa University was established in 1985 in Karaikudi, Tamil Nadu, founded by Dr. R.M. Alagappa Chettiar, and spans a 440-acre campus with 39 departments, 2 constituent colleges, and 9 centres. Its distance-education arm began in 1992 as the Directorate of Distance Education (DDE); per UGC-DEB Regulations 2020, it was renamed the **Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE)**, and now operates two explicitly distinct wings: **Open and Distance Learning (ODL)** and **Online Learning (OL)** — these are not interchangeable, and program availability may differ between the two (see flags).

## Admission Process

1. Apply via the Alagappa University official website or at an authorized Learning Centre
2. Two admission cycles per year: **July–December** (academic year) and **January–June** (calendar year)
3. MBA admission is direct/merit-based — no entrance exam or minimum percentage cutoff
4. Create ABC and DEB IDs as required by UGC guidelines
5. Send the completed form with documents to the Director, CDOE

⚠️ A specific deadline of **30 June 2026** was found for a distance B.Ed admission cycle (per DistanceAdmissions) — treat as a snapshot rather than a standing date.
