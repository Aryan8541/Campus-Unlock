## About

The University of Mumbai's distance education arm — the **Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE)**, formerly known as the **Institute of Distance and Open Learning (IDOL)** — is one of India's largest and oldest distance education providers, established in **1971**. It operates from the Kalina (Vidyanagari) campus in Mumbai and serves over **80,000 enrolled students annually**, making it one of the highest-enrollment distance education divisions in India.

The University of Mumbai was established in **1857** as one of India's three original universities (along with the Universities of Calcutta and Madras), by the same Act of British Indian Legislature. It affiliates over **900 colleges** across Maharashtra and offers programs through 56 teaching departments and 12 specialized centres. The university holds a notable distinction: it ranks **1st among Indian State Universities** in the QS World Graduate Employability Rankings (confirmed by Shiksha).

The CDOE/IDOL is fully UGC-DEB approved and offers programs in arts, science, commerce, and management. The official IDOL Twitter announcement from the university (June 2024) confirmed the transition from the IDOL brand to CDOE, though IDOL remains widely used as an informal name. The admission portal is **mucdoeadm.samarth.edu.in**.

## Admission Process

Confirmed via DistanceAdmissions (July 2026), EducationDunia (July 2026), and CollegePortal:

1. Create ABC ID and DEB ID (mandatory per UGC-DEB guidelines)
2. Visit official CDOE admission portal: **mucdoeadm.samarth.edu.in**
3. Register and fill online application form
4. Upload required documents
5. Pay application fee online (₹300 General / ₹150 Reserved — confirm on portal)
6. Merit-based selection for BA, B.Com, MA, M.Com, MSc programs
7. **MCA and MMS require entrance exam** — this is explicitly confirmed by DistanceAdmissions and DistanceDegreesFYD
8. Enrollment confirmation and LMS access (idoloa.digitaluniversity.ac)

**Admission cycles:** July (primary) and January sessions — two per year.
**Last date July 2026 session:** 31 July 2026 — confirmed by EducationDunia and DistanceAdmissions.
**Pre-registration opened:** 28 May 2026 (EducationDunia).

## ⚠️ Flags — things to double-check before publishing

1. **NAAC CGPA discrepancy: 3.65 vs 3.61.** The official @Uni_Mumbai Twitter account (August 31, 2021) and Careers360 news both state CGPA **3.65**. One aggregator (EducationIConnect) cites 3.61. The official university source takes clear precedence — use 3.65. This is the highest-confidence NAAC figure in this file; no further verification needed.

2. **NIRF rank conflict — resolved as #54 University (NIRF 2025).** Multiple aggregators cite inconsistent ranks: #12 (Shiksha — this is the State Public University rank, not University category), #37 (NIRF 2024 University), #54 (CollegeDekho NIRF 2025 University category), #61 (VidyaLive — unverified), #92 (Overall category). The correct NIRF 2025 University category rank is **#54** per CollegeDekho's ranking page with published score (52.48). Overall rank is #92. Store `54`.

3. **IDOL → CDOE rebrand must be surfaced in UI copy.** The university officially rebranded in 2024. Search traffic will be high for "IDOL Mumbai University" for years to come. The UI should reference both names: "CDOE (formerly IDOL)" or similar. The DB `name` field and slug use "online" but should be complemented by the IDOL mention in the description.

4. **Application fee conflict (₹300 vs ₹350–₹1,050).** EducationIConnect and DistanceAdmissions give slightly different application fee figures. These may reflect different programs or a fee update. Confirm on mucdoeadm.samarth.edu.in before publishing.

5. **MCA and MMS entrance exam requirement must be clearly marked.** Unlike most distance programs which are merit-based, MCA and MMS at CDOE require entrance exam performance. This distinction must be explicit in UI copy — a student applying to MMS expecting no entrance exam (as they might for MA or M.Com) would have a bad experience.

6. **Top recruiter and placement average figures are single-source, low confidence.** The TCS/Infosys/HDFC claim and ₹3–6 LPA average from EducationIConnect cannot be independently verified. Do not publish these in the `top_recruiters` or `average_package` DB fields. `placement_support: True` is defensible (CDOE explicitly mentions placement infrastructure), but specific stats need official confirmation.
