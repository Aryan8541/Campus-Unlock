## About

Osmania University was established in 1918 as the first university in the former Hyderabad State and is the seventh-oldest university in India (third-oldest in South India). It is notable for having historically introduced Urdu as a medium of instruction, with English as a compulsory subject. Its distance-education wing, the **Prof. G. Ram Reddy Centre for Distance Education**, was established in **1977** — making it, alongside Pondicherry's DDE (1995) and JMI's CDOL (2002), one of the longer-established distance-education operations profiled across this research series, and notably the oldest of the four universities in this particular batch.
