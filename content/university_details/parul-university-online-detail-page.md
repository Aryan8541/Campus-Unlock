## About

Parul University Online is the online-learning division of Parul University, a private multidisciplinary university located in Vadodara, Gujarat. The university traces its roots to 1989, when the Parul Arogya Seva Mandal (PASM) Trust was founded, and its first academic institution — Ahmedabad Homeopathic College — opened in 1993 under the trust's umbrella (Parul Group of Institutes). University status was granted on 26 March 2015 by the Government of Gujarat under the Gujarat Private Universities Act.

The online division operates under the Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE) — confirmed by the UGC-DEB disclosure — and received UGC-DEB approval in 2023. Parul University made national headlines as the youngest private university in India to achieve NAAC A++ accreditation in its first assessment cycle (2023, CGPA 3.55, valid to February 2028). The online portal is at **paruluniversity.online**; the parent university's main site is **paruluniversity.ac.in**.

## ⚠️ Flags — things to double-check before publishing

1. **Founding year ambiguity:** Three legitimate dates exist — 1989 (trust), 1993 (first academic institution), 2015 (university status). The UGC-DEB disclosure itself says "Apr 2015" and that is what the DB `established_year` field should hold. The 1993 date belongs in the narrative description, not the `established_year` field. The existing DB had `established_year: None` — this is now fillable with confidence.

2. **NIRF ranking is a band, not an integer:** The DB has a `ranking (int, NIRF)` field, but NIRF reports Parul in the 101–150 band, not a single number. Options: store `101` (lower bound), `125` (midpoint), or store `None` and surface the band in the UI as free text. The NIRF 2024 DEB disclosure section itself shows "NotApplicable" for 2020–2022, confirming they were not in NIRF rankings at all in those years. The 101–150 band placement started only in 2024.

3. **Fees are aggregator-sourced, not official:** The per-semester/total fee figures in the Programs table are drawn from 1–2 aggregators each. The MBA fee (~₹90,000 total) has the strongest corroboration (2 agreeing sources). All others are single-source. Strongly recommend confirming against the official paruluniversity.online site before publishing specific fee figures.

4. **Placement data is entirely on-campus:** No online-specific placement percentage, average package, or named recruiters were found. Everything circulating (₹60 LPA highest, ₹4.5–6 LPA average, TCS/Wipro etc.) is for the on-campus university. The `highest_package` and `average_package` DB fields should remain `None` for the online division.

5. **Scholarships: not confirmed for online students.** The three main scholarship schemes (Sports, Cultural, Defence) are confirmed for regular/on-campus programs. The official online site does not mention any of these applying to online students. Leave the `Scholarship` model empty until confirmed.

6. **WES: do not mark as approved.** The official paruluniversity.online site mentions WES only in a general FAQ answer about Indian online degrees, not as a stated institutional recognition.
