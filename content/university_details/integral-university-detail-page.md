## About

Integral University (IUL) is a private university in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh. Its roots go back to the Institute of Integral Technology (founded 1998), which was elevated to full university status in 2004 under an act of the Uttar Pradesh state legislature — Wikipedia describes it as the first enacted minority university in India (see flag below on this claim). The university runs its online and distance-learning programs through its Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE, iulonline.in), offering UGC-DEB approved undergraduate and postgraduate degrees across IT, management, commerce, and humanities.

## Admission Process

1. Apply via the official CDOE portal (iulonline.in)
2. Merit-based — no entrance exam for the online programs (this differs from Integral's on-campus admissions, which use IUET and other national exams; don't conflate the two)
3. Eligibility check: Class 12 pass for UG programs, graduation for PG programs, no stated age restriction
4. Document submission and fee payment
5. LMS access provided — described as offering live sessions, recorded lectures, study materials, and 24×7 support
