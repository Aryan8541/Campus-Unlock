## About

Guru Ghasidas Vishwavidyalaya (GGV), formerly Guru Ghasidas University, is a Central University in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh, formally inaugurated on 16 June 1983 and later brought under the Central Universities Act, 2009. It is named after the Satnami saint Guru Ghasidas. The university runs its online and distance-learning programs through the Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE), which is UGC-DEB approved and offers a range of undergraduate and postgraduate degrees across commerce, computer applications, library science, humanities, social work, and management.
