## About

Jaypee Institute of Information Technology (JIIT), Noida was founded in 2001 by the Jaypee Group and commenced academic operations that same year, initially affiliated with the state university Jaypee University of Information Technology (JUIT), Waknaghat, Himachal Pradesh. It was declared a Deemed University by the UGC on 1 November 2004. JIIT now offers an online MBA under UGC-DEB approval, alongside its long-established on-campus Engineering, Science, and Management programmes.
