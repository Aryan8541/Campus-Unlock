## About

The University of Mysore, established on 27 July 1916, is one of India's oldest universities and the first to be established outside British India — a genuinely distinctive historical claim, well-corroborated by 2 independent sources. It was also the first university in the country to be accredited by NAAC, receiving a five-star rating in 2000. Its Directorate of Distance Education / Directorate of Online Programmes now offers UGC-DEB approved online and distance degrees across management, commerce, and computer applications, serving a large student base (85,000+ candidates, per one source, including students from abroad).
