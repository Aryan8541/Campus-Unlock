## About

Aligarh Muslim University Online operates through the **Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE)** — the dedicated distance and online learning division of Aligarh Muslim University (AMU), a central public university established in 1920 by an Act of Parliament and designated as an **Institution of National Importance** under the Seventh Schedule, Article 246 of the Indian Constitution.

AMU traces its origins to 1875, when Sir Syed Ahmad Khan founded the Muhammadan Anglo-Oriental (MAO) College in Aligarh to promote modern scientific education among Indian Muslims. The MAO College was elevated to university status in 1920. The university is spread across a 467-hectare residential campus in Aligarh, Uttar Pradesh, and operates three off-campus centres — in Malappuram (Kerala), Kishanganj (Bihar), and Murshidabad (West Bengal).

The CDOE offers UGC-DEB approved online and distance programs at UG, PG, Diploma, and Certificate levels, primarily in Arts, Commerce, and Humanities. AMU's online programs are specifically noted for their commitment to flexible, affordable education for working professionals, women, and educationally backward communities.

## Admission Process

Confirmed via CollegeDunia (CDOE admission page) and multiple aggregators:

1. Visit official CDOE portal: **cdoeamu.ac.in/online-courses**
2. Register and complete online application form
3. Upload required documents
4. Pay application fee of ₹300 (online or at CDOE cash counter on campus)
5. Merit-based selection — no entrance exam required for most programs
6. Enrollment confirmation and LMS access provided

**Admission cycles:** July/August and January/February (two per year).
