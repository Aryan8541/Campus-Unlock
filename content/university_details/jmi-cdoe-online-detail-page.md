## About

Jamia Millia Islamia was founded in 1920 — the name itself translates roughly to "National University" ("Jamia" meaning university, "Millia" meaning national) — and became a Central University under an Act of the Indian Parliament in 1988. Its distance-education arm was established in September 2002 as the **Centre for Distance and Open Learning (CDOL)**, and has since been renamed the **Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE)** — consistently described this way across multiple sources, giving more confidence in this naming transition than some other renaming/restructuring ambiguities seen elsewhere in this research series (e.g., DMIHER's CDOE founding-year uncertainty). CDOE offers undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, and certificate programs across disciplines including Arts and Humanities, Commerce, Management, and Urdu-language studies, and one source specifically notes JMI began offering distinctly **online** (as opposed to traditional print/postal distance) degrees starting in 2021.

## Admission Process

1. Check eligibility criteria for the specific program via the CDOL/CDOE prospectus or official website
2. Complete the application form with personal information, educational qualifications, and other required details (available online via the official CDOL/CDOE portal)
3. Submit required documents — mark sheets, degrees, certificates, identity proof, and photographs
4. Pay the non-refundable application fee online through the university's payment gateway (₹500 specifically cited for PG program applications by one source)
5. Admissions run on cycles roughly twice yearly — January and July sessions, per multiple sources
