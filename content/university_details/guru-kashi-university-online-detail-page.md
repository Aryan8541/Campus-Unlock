## About

Guru Kashi University (GKU) traces its origins to 1997, when the Balaji Education Trust in Talwandi Sabo established Guru Gobind Singh Polytechnic College (1998), followed by Guru Gobind Singh College of Engineering & Technology (2001), Guru Gobind Singh College of Education (2005), and GGS Institute of IT and Research alongside GGS College of Nursing (2006). The university itself was formally constituted in **2011** under the Guru Kashi University Act (Punjab Act No. 37 of 2011) — a state legislative act, distinct from the Section 3 "deemed university" route several other institutions in this project follow. Its Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE) offers UGC-DEB approved online undergraduate and postgraduate degrees.
