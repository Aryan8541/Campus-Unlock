## About

Jawaharlal Nehru University is a central university located in New Delhi, founded in 1969, offering 179+ courses across undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral levels — but exclusively through on-campus, residential study. Multiple sources confirm JNU has **no Centre for Distance Education or ODL school**, and all its UG, PG, MPhil, and PhD programs require classroom attendance and campus residence in New Delhi. JNU should not be confused with **Jaipur National University** (also sometimes abbreviated "JNU"), a separate private university in Jaipur, Rajasthan, that genuinely does offer distance-mode programs like BA (General) — this is very likely the source of the confusion driving the aggregator claims described above.
