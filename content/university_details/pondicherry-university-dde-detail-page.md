## About

Pondicherry University was established as a Central University in 1985, on an 800-acre campus along the Bay of Bengal in Puducherry, and operates 40+ academic departments across Humanities, Sciences, Management, Engineering, Law, Education, and Medical Sciences. It is a key academic institution serving the wider union-territory region, including Puducherry, Lakshadweep, and the Andaman & Nicobar Islands. Its **Directorate of Distance Education (DDE)** was launched in the 1995–96 academic year — one of the longer-running distance-education operations among the universities profiled across this entire research series — and has, per one source, educated over 200,000–260,000 students to date (exact cumulative figure varies slightly by source, see flags). DDE offers UG and PG programs in Arts, Commerce, and Management, with its flagship offering being a wide-specialization MBA program.

## Admission Process

1. Visit the official DDE portal (dde.pondiuni.edu.in) and download the application form, or apply online, per program
2. Fill in the form accurately, including the correct course code
3. Attach required documents
4. Pay the application fee — **₹200**, per two independent sources (a rare point of clean agreement across sources in this research series)
5. Submit via registered post to the DDE office (for offline applications) or complete online submission, before the stated deadline
6. Admission windows run roughly twice yearly — January/February and July/August, per multiple sources, with specific historical deadlines cited (e.g., 12 January 2026 and 15 April 2026 across different application cycles/sources)
7. Most programs are merit-based with **no entrance exam**; the MBA program is the notable exception, accepting CAT scores
