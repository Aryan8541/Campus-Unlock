## About

Symbiosis International (Deemed University), commonly SIU, was founded in Pune in 1971 by Dr. S. B. Mujumdar, originally to support international students, and was granted Deemed-to-be-University status by the Ministry of Human Resource Development in 2002. Symbiosis runs online and distance offerings through **two separate divisions** worth telling apart clearly: the Symbiosis Centre for Distance Learning (SCDL), an older division known mainly for diplomas and certificate programs, and the Symbiosis School for Online and Digital Learning (SSODL), launched in 2018, which is the division offering UGC-DEB approved online **degree** programs (UG and PG) and is the more relevant one for this platform.

## Highlights

- UGC-DEB approved for online UG & PG degree programs under UGC (DEB) Regulations, 2020 — confirmed directly on the official ssodl.edu.in domain
- AICTE approval specifically confirmed for the online MBA
- NAAC A++ accredited, UGC Category-I status (both stated on the official siu.edu.in domain)
- ~22 online UG, PG, Diploma, and Certificate programs across Business, Technology, Humanities, and Healthcare
- 80,000+ online learners and a 12 lakh+ alumni network claimed by one aggregator for SSODL specifically (not independently verified)

## Admission Process

1. Apply via the official SSODL portal (ssodl.edu.in)
2. Merit-based — no entrance exam (no SNAP, group discussion, or personal interview required for the online MBA specifically, per two independent sources)
3. Registration fee payment (₹750–₹1,500 depending on program/source)
4. Document upload and verification
5. Program fee payment to confirm seat, then LMS access provided
