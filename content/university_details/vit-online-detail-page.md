## About

Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT) was founded in 1984 to make innovative higher technical education accessible, and has since expanded into a multi-campus institution with wings in Bangalore, Andhra Pradesh, Bhopal, and Chennai alongside its original Vellore campus. In 2019, VIT launched the VIT Online Learning Institute (VITOL) to offer flexible online postgraduate education for working professionals. VITOL's programs are approved by UGC-DEB and AICTE, and are additionally recognized by World Education Services (WES), the Association to Advance Collegiate Schools of Business (AACSB), and the Accreditation Council for Business Schools and Programs (ACBSP) — a notably dense set of international accreditations compared to most other universities profiled in this research series.
