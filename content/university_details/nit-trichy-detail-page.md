## About

NIT Trichy was founded in 1964 as a joint venture between the Government of India and the Government of Tamil Nadu, originally as Regional Engineering College, and was renamed National Institute of Technology in 2003. Under the NITSER Act, 2007, it was declared an Institute of National Importance. It offers on-campus BTech, BArch, MTech, MSc, MCA, MA, MBA, and PhD programs across Engineering, Architecture, Computer Applications, Management, and Humanities/Social Sciences, admitting students through JEE Main, GATE, NIMCET, and CAT/MAT respectively — all residential, full-time programs. Its **Continuing Education Centre** (referenced by one source in the context of AICTE's Quality Improvement Programme, QIP) organizes short-term courses and faculty-training programmes; a separate source references NIT Trichy "online programs" in energy conversion, machine learning, and similar topics, delivered by faculty from NITs, IITs, and IISc — these read as **short online certificate/training courses, not full UGC-DEB entitled degree programs**.
