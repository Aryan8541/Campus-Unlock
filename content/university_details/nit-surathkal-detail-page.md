## About

NIT Surathkal was established in 1960 and upgraded to National Institute of Technology Karnataka (NITK) status in 2002. It offers UG, PG, and PhD-level programs across engineering, management, science, and information technology, admitting students through JEE Main (BTech), and GATE/CAT/MAT/NIMCET for postgraduate programs (MTech, MBA, MCA, MSc). Its **Centre for Continuing Education (CCE)**, established in **1987** — directly confirmed on the official nitk.ac.in domain — has organized approximately 326 programmes to date, catering to working professionals from academic institutions, government organizations, industries, and commercial banks, drawing on both NITK faculty and, at times, external resource persons. This is a genuinely long-running unit (comparable in longevity to some of the distance-education directorates profiled for conventional universities elsewhere in this research series), but it is explicitly a **short-term/extension-course and workshop operation, not a degree-granting distance-education wing**.
