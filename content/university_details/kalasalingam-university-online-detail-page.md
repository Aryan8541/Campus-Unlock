## About

Kalasalingam Academy of Research and Education (KARE), formerly Arulmigu Kalasalingam College of Engineering (AKCE) and later Kalasalingam University, was founded in 1984 by Kalvivallal Thiru T. Kalasalingam and granted deemed-to-be university status in 2006. Its Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE), established in 2022 per the university's own official page, offers Open Distance Learning (ODL) and Online Learning (OL) programmes at the UG and PG levels.

## Highlights

- CDOE explicitly established in **2022** "with the objective of expanding access to quality higher education through flexible and technology-enabled learning modes" — a clean, specific, official-source claim
- All CDOE programmes are UGC-DEB entitled and AICTE approved, per the official CDOE page
- ⚠️ **NAAC grade needs careful handling — see flags.** An official UGC-DEB exemption PDF states NAAC **Grade A, CGPA 3.11**, valid from 15 November 2020 (second cycle). Several aggregators cite this identical 3.11 CGPA but label it **"A++"** instead of "A" — under NAAC's standard scale, a CGPA of 3.11 falls within the "A" band (3.01–3.25), not "A++" (3.51–4.00), so the "A++" label appears to be a mislabeling error being repeated across multiple aggregator sites rather than a genuine separate, higher accreditation
- 6 engineering programmes accredited by NBA (Tier-1), and 11 engineering departments hold ABET accreditation — both on-campus-only distinctions, not relevant to the online catalog
- One aggregator (UniversityKart) claims a UGC "12(B) status" grant in 2022 — this status category is typically for non-deemed institutions receiving UGC grants and sits oddly alongside KARE's Section 3 deemed-university status since 2006; likely an aggregator error rather than a genuine additional recognition (see flags)
