## About

VISTAS (Vels Institute of Science, Technology & Advanced Studies), commonly known as Vels University, traces back to 1992 as a group of individual colleges (Vels College of Science, Vels College of Pharmacy, and others) before being declared a Deemed-to-be University by the Ministry of Education in 2008. Its online arm, VISTAS Online, is a newer initiative — and appears to currently offer a narrower catalog than most universities in this batch, built around its flagship Online M.Sc. in Data Science & Business Analytics, rather than a broad MBA/BBA/BCA/MCA suite.
