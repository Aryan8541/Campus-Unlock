## ⚠️ Read this before anything else — this one is different from IIT Delhi and IIT Bombay

Unlike IIT Delhi and IIT Bombay (profiled elsewhere in this project, both of which only offer online *Diplomas*), **IIT Madras's BS in Data Science and Applications is a genuine, full, degree-level program** — a real Bachelor of Science conferred by IIT Madras, not a diploma or certificate. It is also **the largest and most established program of its kind from any IIT**, with 36,000+ students and 2.58 lakh+ applications received between January 2021 and January 2026. The same UGC/IIT-jurisdiction nuance from the IIT Delhi/Bombay pages still applies (IITs fall outside UGC's purview and don't need "UGC-DEB approval"), but here it's paired with a genuinely open-enrollment, degree-granting program — a different situation from a diploma-only offering, and worth presenting with real confidence rather than the heavy caveats needed for IIT Delhi/Bombay.

One caution: IIT Madras also offers a **separate BS Electronic Systems program that requires JEE Advanced** — a meaningfully different (and more restrictive) admission path than the Data Science program's open qualifier-exam route. Don't let the Data Science program's openness bleed into how this second program gets described.

## About

IIT Madras launched what it describes as the world's first fully online 4-year Bachelor of Science degree in 2020-21: the BS in Data Science and Applications (originally BSc in Programming and Data Science). Delivered through its own portal (onlinedegree.iitm.ac.in) and its Centre for Outreach and Digital Education (IITM CODE), the program is structured in four levels — Foundation, Diploma, BSc, and BS — with the option to exit at any level. Since launch, IIT Madras has expanded this model to additional programs, including BS in Electronic Systems and BS in Management and Data Science.

## Highlights

- Genuine full Bachelor's degree, not a diploma — the first of its kind from any IIT
- No JEE required for the flagship Data Science program — open to anyone who has completed or is appearing for Class 12, with English and Mathematics at Class 10 level, via a qualifier process
- 36,000+ students currently enrolled; 2.58 lakh+ applications received (Jan 2021–Jan 2026)
- Up to 75% fee waivers for learners earning under ₹5 LPA — 2,000+ students reportedly studying for free
- No age limit — enrolled learners range from 18 to 75+ years old
- Four-level exit structure (Foundation Certificate → Diploma → BSc → BS) aligned with NEP 2020's multiple-exit philosophy
- NAAC A+ (institutional accreditation for IIT Madras overall)

## Admission Process

**BS in Data Science and Applications (and similarly-structured programs):**
1. Register and complete the qualifier process — a 4-week foundational course culminating in in-person quizzes/exams (content is online, but exams are proctored in person)
2. No JEE required for regular entry; JEE Advanced qualifiers get an accelerated path after completing the same 4-week foundation period
3. Progress through Foundation → Diploma → BSc → BS levels, with the option to exit and receive a credential at any stage

**BS Electronic Systems:** requires clearing JEE Advanced — a separate and more restrictive process; don't apply the Data Science program's open-admission language to this one.
