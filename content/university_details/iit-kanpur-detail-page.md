## ⚠️ Read this before anything else — IIT Kanpur has TWO separate, differently-run online postgraduate offerings

This is worth calling out clearly, similar in spirit to the ARKA JAIN and Symbiosis "two portal" situations flagged elsewhere in this project, but more consequential here given both are genuine degree programs from the same institute:

1. **eMasters** (emasters.iitk.ac.in) — launched January 2022. Delivered via a **third-party platform, iPearl.ai, powered by TalentSprint**. Explicitly targeted at working professionals; no thesis component; explicitly **not equivalent to an on-campus M.Tech** (IIT Kanpur's own FAQ states this directly). No GATE/JAM score required.
2. **Online M.Tech / Online M.Sc / Online PG Diploma** (online.iitk.ac.in), run by IIT Kanpur's Office of Outreach Activities — a **separate, newer initiative** (second admissions cycle launched 2026). IIT Kanpur states this is delivered with **"no third-party involvement"** and that these programs maintain **"the same content, structure, and evaluation as on-campus courses, governed by IIT Kanpur's Senate"** — a notably stronger equivalence claim than eMasters makes for itself. Requires an IITK-run online entrance test (some candidates exempted), and at least 50% of the final grade comes from in-person proctored exams at approved test centres.

**These are not interchangeable, and some subject areas (e.g., Data Science) may exist under both umbrellas with different admission processes, different delivery platforms, and different degree framing.** Recommend clarifying which program any specific listing refers to before publishing, and not defaulting to whichever one is easier to find data for.

**The recurring IIT-jurisdiction point still applies to both:** per UGC-DEB's own FAQ (and IIT Kanpur's own eMasters FAQ, which states this almost verbatim), IITs fall outside UGC's purview — "UGC-DEB approved" isn't the right framing for either program, though both are explicitly confirmed by IIT Kanpur as valid, Senate-approved degrees.

## About

IIT Kanpur, established in 1959 as India's fourth IIT, has built two separate online postgraduate offerings. eMasters, launched in January 2022, is explicitly designed as a work-integrated program for industry professionals, delivered through a third-party digital learning platform (iPearl.ai) and covering 10 specialized domains including Data Science and Business Analytics, Cybersecurity, and Financial Technology. Separately, IIT Kanpur's Office of Outreach Activities runs its own Online M.Tech, M.Sc, and PG Diploma programs directly — with no third-party platform involved — positioned as closely mirroring the on-campus degree experience, including Senate governance and in-person proctored exams.

## Highlights

- Two genuine, IIT Kanpur Senate-approved degree pathways, each with a different design philosophy
- eMasters explicitly states it grants formal degrees at convocation, not "diploma or professional course" credentials
- No GATE/JAM score required for either eMasters or (for exam-exempted candidates) the newer Online M.Tech/M.Sc track
- eMasters reached 1,000+ enrollments as a milestone (reported around its expansion to 10 specialized programs)
- The newer Online M.Tech/M.Sc/PG Diploma track explicitly states its coursework is 85%+ developed by IIT Kanpur itself, with the remainder involving "selective industry inputs"
- Credits from either track can potentially transfer toward an advanced degree (regular M.Tech or PhD) at IIT Kanpur, subject to Senate approval — confirmed for eMasters specifically

## Admission Process

**eMasters:** Apply via emasters.iitk.ac.in; eligibility review based on academic background and work experience; no GATE/JAM required.

**Online M.Tech/M.Sc/PG Diploma:** Apply via online.iitk.ac.in; most candidates must clear the IITK online entrance test; some categories (e.g., government/defense-sponsored candidates) may be exempted; at least 50% of final grades come from in-person proctored exams at approved test centres (or secure online proctoring for candidates outside India); application fee cited as ₹15,000 (₹10,000 refundable if admission isn't offered).

## Learning Methodology
