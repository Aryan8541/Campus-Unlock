## ⚠️ STRUCTURAL NOTE — Trifurcation of Bangalore University

**Bangalore University was trifurcated in 2020** by the Karnataka Government into three separate universities:
1. **Bangalore University** (retained) — Jnana Bharathi campus, Bengaluru South
2. **Bengaluru City University** (new) — covers Bengaluru urban areas
3. **Bengaluru North University** (new) — covers northern districts

This is confirmed by Wikipedia and multiple sources. The CDOE/DDE operates under **Bangalore University (the retained entity)** at the Jnana Bharathi campus. ## About

Bangalore University's distance and online education division — the **Centre for Distance & Online Education (CDOE)**, formerly the Directorate of Distance Education (DDE) — is one of Karnataka's oldest distance learning providers, having been established in **1976**, making it among the earliest distance education divisions of any state university in South India.

Bangalore University itself was established in **1964**, when it was bifurcated from the University of Mysore. It is a public state university under the Government of Karnataka, located on the 1,100-acre Jnana Bharathi campus in Bengaluru. Following the 2020 trifurcation, it now directly governs its constituent colleges and departments while the CDOE continues to serve distance and online learners across Karnataka and beyond.

The CDOE received UGC recognition to offer programs in online mode from **2023–24** — an important milestone distinguishing the newer "online mode" programs from the older correspondence/ODL programs. As per the official CDOE prospectus (2024–25 onwards), all UG and PG programs are now offered in online mode for the 2026 academic session as per UGC guidelines.

## Admission Process

Confirmed via official CDOE prospectus PDF and multiple aggregators:

1. Create ABC ID and DEB ID (mandatory for UGC-DEB institutions — required before applying)
2. Register on the official CDOE portal: **ddebubonline.in**
3. Fill in personal and program details
4. Upload scanned documents (coloured)
5. Pay processing/application fee online (₹500–₹1,000 depending on program)
6. Receive email confirmation post document verification
7. Login to LMS using registered mobile number

**No entrance exam** — all CDOE admissions are merit-based on qualifying examination marks. Confirmed across 5+ sources.

**Expected last date July 2026 session:** 30 July 2026 (confirmed across 3 aggregators).
**Fees payable in two installments** per year — before semester start and before examinations (confirmed by icdde.com).
