## About

The University of Delhi (DU) is a central public university established in 1922, one of India's largest and most reputed universities, with 91 affiliated colleges and a student body exceeding 7 lakh across all its programs. Its distance and online-learning arm, the School of Open Learning (SOL), was established in 1962 — starting with 900 students across five courses — and has grown into one of India's largest open-learning institutions, with roughly 5 lakh currently enrolled students. SOL is UGC-DEB approved and offers undergraduate and postgraduate degrees, primarily in Arts and Commerce, with wide accessibility and low fees relative to most private online universities.

## Highlights

- UGC-DEB approved — validated by the University Grants Commission's Distance Education Bureau, per the official-adjacent SOL admissions information
- NAAC grade conflict found — most sources say A+ (matching the main University of Delhi's own accreditation), though a couple of SOL-specific aggregators state A++ instead; see flags
- One of India's largest distance-learning institutions by enrollment — approximately 5 lakh students
- Degrees awarded through SOL are stated to carry equal academic validity and professional recognition to those from a regular DU college
- No CUET or other entrance exam required for SOL admission — one of the few CUET-free pathways into a DU degree
- Very low fees relative to most other universities profiled on this platform

## Admission Process

1. Apply via the official SOL admission portal
2. No CUET or other entrance exam required — this is one of the only CUET-free routes to a University of Delhi degree (the other being NCWEB, for female candidates in select programs)
3. Create an ABC ID and DEB ID per current UGC-wide requirements for ODL/online enrollment (effective September 2024)
4. Document upload and verification
5. Fee payment and enrollment confirmation

## Learning Methodology
