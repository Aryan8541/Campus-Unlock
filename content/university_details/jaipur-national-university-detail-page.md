## About

Jaipur National University (JNU — not to be confused with Jawaharlal Nehru University, Delhi) is a self-financed private university established on October 22, 2007, via an ordinance of the Government of Rajasthan, with UGC recognition under Section 2(f) granted in 2008. The university runs its distance/online offerings through the School of Open Learning (SODEL), which launched around 2008-09, and more recently through a dedicated Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE) portal.

## Highlights

- UGC-DEB approved for online-mode programs — confirmed on the official online.jnujaipur.ac.in domain
- Described as among the first private universities in Rajasthan to receive NAAC accreditation
- AIU member — stated on the official jnujaipur.ac.in accreditation page
- Broad online catalog: 21 courses across UG, PG, and Diploma levels in IT, Computer Applications, Management, and Data Science (per one aggregator)
- AICTE-approved technical/professional programs at the university level

## Admission Process

1. Apply via the official online portal (online.jnujaipur.ac.in)
2. Merit-based — no entrance exam for the online/SODEL programs specifically (this differs from JNU's on-campus admissions, which use JEE Main, NEET, CAT, CLAT, and other national exams; don't conflate the two)
3. Document submission and fee payment
4. LMS access — recorded lectures, live sessions, e-learning materials, and discussion forums (per one aggregator)
