## About

IIIT-Delhi (IIITD) is a state-funded, self-governing technical institute established by the Government of NCT of Delhi, distinct from the federally-funded Indian Institute of Technology Delhi (IIT Delhi) — a name-adjacency worth flagging explicitly, since both institutions are in Delhi, both have "online PG programmes," and casual searching easily conflates the two (see Flags). IIIT-Delhi offers three PG Diploma programmes, run in partnership with external ed-tech/industry providers: a 12-month **PG Diploma in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence** (with Great Learning) and a 9-month **PG Diploma in Data Science & AI** (with IBM). These are structured as live, interactive online classes with career support, mentorship, and a path to IIIT-Delhi alumni status — but they are explicitly diploma-level, industry-ed-tech-partnered credentials, not full UGC-DEB entitled Master's or Bachelor's degree programs of the kind tracked elsewhere in this database.
