## About

The University of Madras' distance education arm — the **Institute of Distance Education (IDE)**, also known as IDEUNOM — is one of India's largest and most established distance education providers, having been set up in **1981** under the parent university. The parent University of Madras was established on **5 September 1857** following a public petition in 1839, modelled on the University of London, and is widely regarded as the "mother of most older South Indian universities." Its historic jurisdiction once extended across the entire Madras Presidency; today it affiliates over 200 colleges across Chennai, Thiruvallur, Chengalpattu, and Kanchipuram districts in Tamil Nadu.

The IDE (IDEUNOM) is fully recognized by UGC-DEB and offers **73 UGC-DEB approved courses** — one of the largest distance education catalogs of any Indian state university — spanning 17 UG, 21 PG, 19 Diploma, and 16 Certificate programs. Admissions operate in two annual cycles (July and January sessions), and programs are offered in English and Tamil medium.

The university received its **NAAC A++ grade (CGPA 3.59) in August 2023** — the highest possible grade — in its fourth accreditation cycle, confirmed directly on the official unom.ac.in/naac page and by a Careers360 news article. The university has held NAAC accreditation continuously since 2000, making it one of Tamil Nadu's most consistently accredited public universities.

## Admission Process

Confirmed via DistanceAdmissions (July 2026), EducationDunia, and icdde.com:

1. Create ABC ID and DEB ID (mandatory per UGC-DEB guidelines)
2. Visit official IDE portal: **ideunom.ac.in** / **www.ideunom.ac.in**
3. Fill online application form
4. Upload required documents
5. Pay application fee of **₹224** online (confirmed by EducationDunia — notably affordable)
6. Merit-based selection for most programs; TANCET/CAT/MAT required for MBA
7. Enrollment confirmation and access to LMS and study materials

**Admission cycles:** July (primary) and January sessions — two per year.
**Last date July 2026 session:** 31 July 2026 — confirmed by DistanceAdmissions and EducationDunia.
