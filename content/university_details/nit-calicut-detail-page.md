## About

NIT Calicut (NITC) was established in 1961 as Regional Engineering College (REC) Kozhikode and upgraded to NIT status in 2002 under the NIT Act. It is a fully residential Institute of National Importance spanning a 120-hectare campus roughly 22 km from Kozhikode, hosting around 7,300 students across 15 departments in engineering, sciences, architecture, and management. Admission to its BTech program runs through JoSAA following JEE Main; MTech through GATE/CCMT; other postgraduate programs through CAT, MAT, or IIT JAM as applicable. Its **Centre for Continuing Education and Skill Development (CCESD)** — directly confirmed on the official nitc.ac.in domain — is described as dedicated to designing, developing, and delivering educational, research, training, and outreach programmes for personal and professional development, in association with various departments and external agencies. **This is explicitly a short-term/skill-development unit, not a UGC-DEB entitled online or distance degree-granting centre.**

**Name-confusion note (high priority):** NIT Calicut must not be confused with the **University of Calicut** (also referred to as "Calicut University"), a separate public state university headquartered in Malappuram, Kerala, established in 1968, with a genuine, well-documented distance-education wing — the **Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE)**, formerly the "Institute of Correspondence Courses and Continuing Education," established in **1981**. The University of Calicut's CDOE is UGC-DEB approved, NAAC A+ (or A++, per one source), reports a NIRF rank around 70th, and offers roughly 25 programs (13 UG, 12 PG) including BA, BSc, BCom, BBA, MA, MCom, and MSc, serving on the order of 50,000 new enrollments per year. **This is a real, substantial, UGC-DEB approved distance-education operation — but it belongs to the University of Calicut, not to NIT Calicut, and the two should never be merged into a single CampusUnlock database entry.**
