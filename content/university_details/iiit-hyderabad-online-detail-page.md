## About

IIIT Hyderabad was established in 1998 as India's first IIIT, structured as a non-profit public-private partnership rather than a government-funded Institute of National Importance (the category NITs and IITs fall under) — this is a meaningful structural distinction, since it places IIIT-H's regulatory status closer to a conventional UGC/AICTE/NAAC-recognized deemed university than to an NIT. It is renowned for research-led education in Computer Science, Electronics, and allied fields, and has historically run well-received **Online Executive Education programs** (short courses, PG certificates, in partnership with providers like TalentSprint) through its Centre-adjacent outreach arm. More recently, IIIT-H has moved into **genuine, full online degree territory**: in **April 2024**, it launched an **Online Master of Science in Information Technology (eMSIT)** in partnership with Coursera — described in a press release as the first master's degree from an Indian university on Coursera — and in **September 2025**, it launched a **UGC-approved Online Master of Science in Data Science (MSc DS)** through its newly-established **Division of Flexible Learning (DFL)**, with the institute's director explicitly stating on record that the program "has been approved by the UGC" and will "provide equivalent standing as any other Masters degree program."

**Flag:** Two distinct online Master's offerings were found — the 2024 Coursera-partnered eMSIT and the 2025 DFL-run MSc Data Science — and it's unclear from the sources reviewed whether the eMSIT program is still active/enrolling, has been superseded by the DFL-run MSc Data Science, or continues to run in parallel as a separate track.
