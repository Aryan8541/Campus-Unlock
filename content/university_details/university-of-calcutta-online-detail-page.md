## About

The University of Calcutta's online and distance education division — the **Directorate of Distance Education** — is the distance learning arm of one of Asia's oldest and most prestigious universities. The University of Calcutta was established on **24 January 1857** under the Act of Incorporation passed by the British Indian Government — making it the first multidisciplinary university in Asia. Its alumni include three Nobel Laureates: Rabindranath Tagore, C.V. Raman, and Amartya Sen.

The university received **UGC-DEB approval to offer online programs from 2022**, a relatively recent milestone for an institution with such a long legacy. Online programs include MA, M.Com, MBA, BA, and B.Com in online mode. The Directorate of Distance Education offers the university's range of programs with the same faculty, curriculum standards, and degree credentials as on-campus programs.

The university is formally recognized by the Government of India as a **"Centre of Excellence"** and **"University with Potential for Excellence"** by UGC — two of the highest recognitions granted to Indian public universities.
