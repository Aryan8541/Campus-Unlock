## About

Chaudhary Charan Singh University (CCSU), formerly Meerut University, is a public state university established in 1965 in Meerut, Uttar Pradesh, named after former Prime Minister Chaudhary Charan Singh. It is one of North India's leading state universities, with a 222-acre campus affiliated with 109 colleges, one constituent college, and 252 professional colleges and institutions. Its Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE) offers UGC-DEB approved online and distance programs — recently expanded following the university's NAAC accreditation. CCSU is the Chancellor-governed by the Governor of Uttar Pradesh.

## Admission Process

1. Apply via the official CDOE portal (ccsuniversitycdoe.com)
2. Two intakes per year: **January** (deadline ~November 30) and **July** (deadline ~May 31 / July 10)
3. Merit-based admission — no entrance exam required for most programs
4. Submit required documents online
5. Fee payment and enrollment confirmation
