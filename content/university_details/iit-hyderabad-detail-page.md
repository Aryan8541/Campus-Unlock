## ⚠️ Read this before anything else

**Two things worth clarifying up front:**

1. **IIT Hyderabad's Online M.Tech/M.Des programs are genuine, work-integrated degree offerings for currently-employed professionals — not open-enrollment programs.** Eligibility explicitly requires a first-class Bachelor's degree (BE/B.Tech/BArch/BDes/equivalent) **plus 2 years of post-qualification industrial experience**, along with an NOC (No Objection Certificate) from the employer and a work-experience certificate at application. This is the same structural pattern as BITS Pilani's WILP and IIT Kanpur's eMasters, both flagged elsewhere in this project — not a program a fresh graduate or career-changer without relevant experience could join.
2. **One aggregator source makes a confusing, likely-misapplied claim** that "M.Tech Online is invalid" in India, seemingly generalizing from the Supreme Court precedent about distance-mode engineering degrees (the same *Vinit Garg v. UGC* precedent found regarding Thapar's distance B.Tech, flagged elsewhere in this project). That precedent concerns **UGC-regulated universities** offering engineering degrees via ODL/distance mode without proper UGC/AICTE approval. IIT Hyderabad's Online M.Tech programs are a different situation entirely — conferred under IIT Hyderabad's own authority as an Institute of National Importance, the same basis every other IIT in this project relies on. The aggregator's blanket statement appears to conflate these two different legal situations, and shouldn't be taken as applying to IIT Hyderabad's actual, currently-running programs.

## About

IIT Hyderabad runs a set of Online M.Tech and M.Des programs specifically designed for working professionals, delivered without requiring a break from employment. The CSE department's M.Tech in Data Science (an extension of an earlier Executive M.Tech in Data Science program) has run since August 2018, while a broader expansion — 7 online M.Tech programs plus an online M.Des — was launched institute-wide in 2021, alongside a dual-degree M.Tech in Techno-Entrepreneurship for IIT Hyderabad's own B.Tech students. Individual departments (e.g., Electrical Engineering, Metallurgical & Materials Engineering) continue to run their own specific online/self-sponsored M.Tech tracks under this umbrella.

## Highlights

- Online M.Tech in Data Science described as "equivalent to regular MTech programme" by its own department page (CSE)
- Multiple department-level online tracks exist (Data Science, Industrial Metallurgy, and others), each with its own specific eligibility and fee structure rather than one institute-wide standard
- No GATE score required for these self-sponsored/executive tracks — admission instead via written test and interview
- Executive M.Tech exit option available in at least one program (Industrial Metallurgy) for candidates who complete coursework but not the full thesis component

## Admission Process

Admission to these programs is generally via a written test and interview — GATE is explicitly not required for the self-sponsored/executive tracks (distinct from IIT Hyderabad's regular on-campus M.Tech, which is GATE-based via COAP). Applicants must submit an NOC from their employer and proof of work experience. Some tracks offer an "Executive M.Tech" exit option for those who complete coursework but not the full thesis/capstone component.
