## About

Gujarat University was established in 1949 in Ahmedabad and provides education across diverse streams including science, commerce, management, medical, and defence studies, per its own official department-listing page. Its online/distance education is delivered through the **Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE)**, offering undergraduate and postgraduate degree programs described as mirroring the academic rigor of the university's traditional, on-campus offerings while providing structural flexibility.

**Flag:** Gujarat University's CDOE should not be confused with **Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar Open University (BAOU)** — a separate, dedicated open university also based in Ahmedabad (established 1994, sometimes called the "Open University of Gujarat"), which independently offers its own UGC-DEB approved distance/online MBA and other programs. The two institutions coexist in the same city, both offer online MBAs, and several of the aggregator sources reviewed in this pass discuss them on the same page/listing (e.g., "Top Online/Distance MBA Universities in Gujarat" pieces), creating a real risk of conflating BAOU's program details, fees, or approvals with Gujarat University's CDOE, or vice versa. **Keep these as two entirely separate CampusUnlock database entries.**
