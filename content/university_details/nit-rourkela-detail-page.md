## About

NIT Rourkela was established in 1961 as Regional Engineering College, Rourkela, and was elevated to National Institute of Technology, Rourkela status in 2002 in recognition of its contribution to engineering, science, and technology education. It offers BTech programs across Mechanical, Electrical, Ceramic, Civil, Chemical, Materials and Metallurgical, Mining, and Computer Science and Engineering, along with MTech, MSc, and MCA postgraduate programs, and doctoral research. Its official website confirms a **Continuing Education** unit (hosting workshops, conferences, and training programmes, per the current events listed on the official site) and — notably — a program specifically designed to let **working professionals pursue a doctorate degree while continuing in their job**, with admission based on academic background, a written test, and/or interview per NIT guidelines. **Neither of these is a UGC-DEB entitled online or distance UG/PG degree program.**
