## ⚠️ Two things to verify before publishing

1. **"UGC-DEB approved" vs. "UGC entitled for Online Education"**: most third-party aggregators describe GEU Online generically as "UGC-DEB approved," but the university's **own official site (geuonline.com)** is more precise, repeatedly stating it is *"approved by UGC for providing Online Education under 'Entitled' category"* — this is the UGC (Online) Regulations 2018 pathway for fully-online degrees, a different regulatory track from the UGC-DEB / Open & Distance Learning (ODL) approval that governs universities like Amity, Andhra, or Chandigarh Online. The university's own mandatory-disclosure page separately references an application "for recognition of ODL and Online-Programmes for 2020-21," suggesting both tracks may be relevant. Recommend using the university's own precise language ("UGC entitled, Online category") rather than the generic "UGC-DEB approved" phrase aggregators default to.
2. **Two related but distinct Graphic Era institutions exist**: **Graphic Era (Deemed to be University)** — GEU, the older institution (deemed-university status granted 2008), and **Graphic Era Hill University** — GEHU, a separate private state university established 2011 with campuses in Bhimtal and Haldwani. GEU Online's own history page and lineage narrative confirms the online programs are run by **GEU (the deemed university)**, not GEHU — but interestingly, GEHU's own "About Us" page makes an identical "only university in Uttarakhand" online-education claim. This mirrors the Manav Rachna two-entity ambiguity flagged in the prior research batch; worth double-checking which entity's name appears on the actual degree certificate.

## About

Graphic Era University Online is the online-learning arm of Graphic Era (Deemed to be University) in Dehradun, Uttarakhand. The institution's roots trace to a computer training center founded in 1993 by Kamal Ghanshala, which grew into Graphic Era Institute of Technology (GEIT, founded 1997) before being granted deemed-university status in 2008. In 2022, GEU launched fully-online UG and PG degree programs, becoming — per both the university's own site and several independent sources — the first and only university in Uttarakhand approved by UGC to do so. GEU Online offers a compact catalog: B.Com (Hons.), BBA, BCA, MBA, and MCA.
