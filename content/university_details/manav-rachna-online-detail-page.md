## ⚠️ Read this before publishing — possible two-entity mix-up

There is a genuine ambiguity worth resolving before this page goes live: sources describe what may be **two distinct but related institutions** under the "Manav Rachna" umbrella (both part of Manav Rachna Educational Institutions / MREI):

- **Manav Rachna International Institute of Research and Studies (MRIIRS)** — per Wikipedia, a **deemed university**, formerly "Manav Rachna International University," established 1997, located in Faridabad.
- **Manav Rachna University (MRU)** — per multiple aggregators (CollegeDekho, CollegeBatch), a **private state university**, established 2004 as an engineering college and granted university status by UGC in 2014.

Manav Rachna Online's own site (manavrachnaonline.com) states its degrees are delivered through **MRIIRS** specifically, while most third-party aggregator content about "Manav Rachna Online"/"Manav Rachna University Online" describes **MRU**. These may be legally distinct entities with different UGC recognition histories. Recommend confirming with certainty which entity's name and recognition should appear on the actual degree certificate before publishing.

## About

Manav Rachna Online is the online-learning arm of the Manav Rachna Educational Institutions (MREI) group in Faridabad, Haryana, delivering degrees through a Centre for Distance and Online Education. The group's roots go back to 1997. Manav Rachna Online positions itself as "industry-integrated," citing co-developed curricula (e.g., MCA/BCA content developed with Microsoft and Xebia, per one aggregator — not independently corroborated elsewhere) alongside standard UGC-DEB approved online UG/PG programs in business, commerce, computer applications, and journalism.
