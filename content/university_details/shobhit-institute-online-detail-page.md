## About

Shobhit Institute of Engineering & Technology (SIET), popularly known as Shobhit University Meerut, was granted Deemed-to-be University status under Section 3 of the UGC Act in November 2006. Its Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE/ODL) offers UGC-DEB approved undergraduate and postgraduate programmes in management, computer applications, and commerce. ⚠️ **This is a distinct legal entity from "Shobhit University," Gangoh (Saharanpur)** — a separate state-legislated private university established in 2011 under a different UP state act, not a deemed university, and not the same institution as this one, despite the shared "Shobhit" branding (see flags).

## Highlights

- NAAC accredited **Grade A, CGPA 3.12** (on a 4-point scale), valid for 5 years from **20 December 2022** (second cycle) — this figure is genuinely consistent across the official site, an official DEB application PDF, and every aggregator checked, with no conflicting grade found (a cleaner case than several other universities in this project)
- All CDOE programmes are UGC-DEB approved, per the official shobhitodl.in site
- ⚠️ **B.Tech in distance/online mode is explicitly *not* UGC-DEB approved** (per CollegeVidya) — a program-specific restriction rather than a blanket institutional gap; the CDOE catalog itself is limited to non-technical UG/PG programmes accordingly

- More than 2,000 recruiters reported visiting campus annually — a university-wide, on-campus statistic, not specific to the online division (see Placements)
