## About

B.S. Abdur Rahman Crescent Institute of Science and Technology is a deemed-to-be university founded in 1984 by B.S. Abdur Rahman, located in Vandalur, Chennai, Tamil Nadu. It has operated under several names since its founding — as B.S. Abdur Rahman Crescent Engineering College (1984–2008), B.S. Abdur Rahman Crescent University (2008–2017), and its current name from 2017. Its Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE) offers online MBA and MCA programs developed in accordance with UGC Online Regulations 2020. The institution has a 50-acre suburban campus and currently enrolls 9,239 students (2024), with 295 academic staff. It also holds the QS I·GAUGE E-LEAD certification recognizing excellence in digital learning.
