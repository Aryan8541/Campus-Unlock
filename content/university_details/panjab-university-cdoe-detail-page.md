## About

Panjab University's distance/online education wing traces back to **1971**, when it was established as the **Directorate of Correspondence Courses** — later renamed the **University School of Open Learning (USOL)**, and now operating as the **Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE)**. This naming lineage (Directorate of Correspondence Courses → USOL → CDOE) is consistently described across multiple sources, giving reasonable confidence in the institutional history, similar to Jamia Millia Islamia's CDOL→CDOE transition described in an earlier batch. Across more than 50 years, CDOE has offered cost-effective, flexible higher education in arts, commerce, management, computer applications, and social sciences, and — per one source — explicitly supports **dual-degree study**, allowing students to complete a regular on-campus course while simultaneously pursuing a second degree through distance/online mode, in line with UGC-DEB norms.

**Flag:** One low-quality source (UmeAcademy) contains an internally contradictory and almost certainly incorrect statement that "Panjab University was established in 1982... [and] launched its online learning platform in the year 1971" — the 1971 date almost certainly refers to the Directorate of Correspondence Courses' founding (consistent with all other sources), not an "online learning platform," and Panjab University itself long predates 1982 (it was originally established in Lahore in 1882 and re-established in Chandigarh in 1947 after Partition, per general historical knowledge, not from a source in this specific pass) — this looks like a garbled, unreliable aggregator statement and should not be used for any founding-year claim.

## Admission Process

1. Create a UGC-DEB unique ID and ABC ID, using email, phone number, and Aadhaar details (a now-standard, UGC-wide requirement seen consistently across this research series)
2. Visit the CDOE admission portal (cdoeadmissions.puexam.in, per one source)
3. Complete the Admission-cum-Examination Form, including selecting a preferred examination center
4. For the MBA (ODL) program specifically: sit the CET entrance exam — 2 hours, 100 multiple-choice questions across four sections (data interpretation, numerical ability, verbal ability, general English), no negative marking, 100% weightage in selection, per one detailed source
5. For all other UG/PG programs: selection is based on performance in the previous qualifying examination (merit-based, no entrance test)
6. Submit personal, educational, address, and contact details accurately — one source specifically warns that incorrect details can lead to application rejection
