## About

Vignan University Online is the online-learning arm of Vignan's Foundation for Science, Technology & Research (VFSTR), a deemed-to-be university in Vadlamudi, Guntur, Andhra Pradesh, operated through its Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE). One source describes the institution's overall legacy as spanning "45 years," which — combined with a variously-cited founding year (see flag) — suggests the Vignan Educational Trust's broader history predates the university's formal deemed-university status. Vignan Online offers a compact catalog of online UG/PG programs, primarily BBA, BCA, MBA, and MCA.

## Highlights

- NAAC A+ accredited, with one source citing a specific CGPA of 3.40 (single-source figure, not corroborated elsewhere)
