## About

ARKA JAIN University (AJU) is a private university in Jamshedpur, Jharkhand, established in 2017 under an act of the Jharkhand state legislature — the first state private university in the Kolhan region. It is part of the wider JAIN Group of Institutions, Bengaluru, and is mentored by JAIN (Deemed-to-be-University), Bengaluru. The university runs its online degree programs through a dedicated portal (onlineaju.ac.in), separate from its Open & Distance Learning (ODL) offerings (odlaju.ac.in) — see flags below on this split.

## Highlights

- UGC-DEB approved for online-mode MBA, BBA, BCA, MCA, and B.Com
- NAAC A Grade — achieved in 2025; described by the university as the first NAAC A-grade private university in Bihar, Jharkhand, and West Bengal
- Mentored by JAIN (Deemed-to-be-University), Bengaluru — NAAC A++, NIRF Top-100

- 60+ national and international MoUs via the JAIN Group network
