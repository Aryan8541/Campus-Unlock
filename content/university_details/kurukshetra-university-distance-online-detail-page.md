## About

Kurukshetra University (KUK) is a state public university founded in 1957, with its foundation stone laid by Dr. Rajendra Prasad, India's first President. Its distance-education arm — historically called the **Directorate of Distance Education (DDE)** and now also referred to as the **Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE)** — was established in 1976 and has grown into one of Haryana's longest-running distance-learning institutions, currently offering around 40 programmes to an enrolment of over 20,000 students (per the official DDE page). The university holds UGC Category I status and was reported to be placed 8th among Category-I State Universities nationally by the Ministry of Education, with associated academic autonomy.

## Highlights

- NAAC re-accredited to **A++ Grade, CGPA 3.56/4**, in its **fourth accreditation cycle (announced February 2024)** — per a Careers360 news report quoting the Vice-Chancellor, this made KUK the **first public-sector university in Haryana** to achieve NAAC's highest grade, and it remains, per the official NIRF page, **the only university in Haryana with an A++ grade**

## Programs, Fees & Eligibility

**Catalog** (per the official DDE page and aggregators): UG, PG, Diploma, and Certificate-level programmes across roughly 40 courses, including BA, B.Com, MBA, MCA, M.Sc., B.Ed, LLM, and BLIS, delivered under UGC-DEB-approved ODL/Online modes.

**Fees:** Two aggregator figures roughly agree, without being identical:
- ₹5,800–₹33,000 (per one source's stated range)
- ₹6,000–₹37,000 (per another source's stated range)

Both describe a similarly-shaped range (low-to-mid four figures up to mid-thirty-thousands), so this looks like normal rounding/snapshot variation rather than a real conflict — but neither source breaks the range down per specific program, so don't attribute a single figure to a named program without checking the official fee circular.

**Application/processing fee:** ₹800 (general category), ₹200 (reserved category) — consistently cited.

Eligibility: a prior three-year degree with a minimum 50% aggregate in the qualifying exam (45% for reserved categories) for most PG programs; B.Ed additionally requires an NCTE-recognised teacher-education pathway via face-to-face components, unlike the university's other fully-distance offerings.

## Admission Process

1. Apply via the official distance-education portal (referenced across sources as **www.ddekuk.ac.in**)
2. Admission is merit-based; no entrance test required for most programs
3. Exception: **B.Ed** requires completing an NCTE-recognised teacher-education programme with a mandatory face-to-face component
4. Pay the processing fee (₹800 general / ₹200 reserved) plus applicable program fees
5. For the July 2026 session: admissions opened **1 July 2026**, with a deadline of **15 September 2026** for most programmes and **1 September 2026** specifically for B.Ed — these two dates were confirmed consistently across two independent sources
