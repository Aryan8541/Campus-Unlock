## About

Karnataka State Open University (KSOU) was established in 1996 as the first Open University in Karnataka, headquartered at Mukthagangothri, Mysuru. It is a state-run public institution, not a private or deemed university, and by a Government of Karnataka Gazette notification (No. DPAL 43 SHASANA 2020, dated 30-12-2020), **no university other than KSOU is legally permitted to offer programmes in Open and Distance Learning mode within the state** — a distinctive regulatory-monopoly status not seen in the other universities profiled so far. KSOU runs its offerings across two separate portals: a broad Open and Distance Learning (ODL) catalog on its main site, and a smaller, separately branded "Online Programme" catalog on its own dedicated online-programme portal (see flags — these are not the same thing).

## Admission Process

KSOU runs two admission cycles per year (January and July). As of this review, the official Online Programme portal shows the **2025-26 July cycle as closed**, with renewal/exam-fee notifications for earlier cycles still being processed — meaning any specific cycle date referenced should be treated as a snapshot, not a standing deadline. A January 2026 cycle notification (dated 07-01-2026) was also found, with the final application deadline "to be announced" at time of that posting.

⚠️ Per UGC guidelines referenced in the official prospectus, **ABC ID and DEB ID creation is mandatory for admission** — a specific procedural requirement not seen flagged this explicitly for the other universities profiled.

General flow reported across sources:
1. Visit the official KSOU portal (main site or the dedicated Online Programme portal, depending on which catalog the program belongs to)
2. Register and complete the online application form
3. Create the mandatory ABC ID and DEB ID
4. Submit required documents and pay the applicable fee
