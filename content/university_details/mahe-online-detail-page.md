## About

Manipal Academy of Higher Education (MAHE), established in 1953, is one of India's most prominent private deemed universities and a Government of India-designated Institution of Eminence. It offers 380+ programs across 36 disciplines on-campus, and delivers UGC-entitled online degree programs through the Online Manipal platform, alongside sibling institutions Manipal University Jaipur and Sikkim Manipal University.
