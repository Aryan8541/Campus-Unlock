## ⚠️ Read this before anything else — a name-confusion risk found in the sources themselves

**IIT Guwahati (Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati) and IIIT Guwahati (Indian Institute of Information Technology Guwahati) are two separate institutions**, and at least one aggregator source found in this research (CollegeVidya's "IIT Guwahati M.Tech" page) appears to describe IIIT Guwahati's Online M.Tech in CSE program while labeling it as "IIT Guwahati" — the program description matches IIIT Guwahati's own official page (iiitg.ac.in) almost word-for-word, including near-identical phrasing about "IT professionals having academic backgrounds" and industry-aligned curriculum design. This looks like a genuine aggregator error, similar in kind to the ARKA JAIN/JAIN Group name-confusion flagged elsewhere in this project. Recommend double-checking any content that names "IIT Guwahati" and "Online M.Tech in CSE" together against IIT Guwahati's own official OES portal (iitg.ac.in/oes) before publishing, since IIT Guwahati's actual, confirmed online postgraduate offerings are different (MTech in Flood & Water Resources Management and MTech in Robotics & AI — see below), not a CSE-specific program.

## About

IIT Guwahati, established in 1994 and the sixth member of the IIT system, runs its online degree programs through a dedicated unit, Outreach Education & Skilling (OES), which explicitly bills itself as "the nodal department for all online educational initiatives at the institute." As of this research, IIT Guwahati's confirmed online degree catalog includes a BSc (Hons) in Data Science & AI (undergraduate, open enrollment), an MTech in Flood & Water Resources Management (FWRM), and a newly-launched MTech in Robotics & AI — each explicitly marketed as carrying "full recognition of IIT Guwahati," identical to the on-campus degree.

## Highlights

- OES's own tagline states these carry "Same Degree as On-Campus" recognition — a strong, explicit equivalence claim
- BSc (Hons) in Data Science & AI — offered through the Mehta Family School of Data Science and Artificial Intelligence — launched in 2023, open to Class 12 graduates with no JEE requirement (though JEE Advanced-eligible candidates get direct admission)
- MTech in Flood & Water Resources Management — GATE not required, no age or location restriction, multi-entry/multi-exit structure
- Also available via Coursera (in addition to IIT Guwahati's own admission portal) for the BSc program specifically
- NIRF Engineering rank cited as 7th (2023, per one aggregator — see Rankings for a caveat on how current this figure is)

## Admission Process

**BSc (Hons) in Data Science & AI:** Apply via opadmission.iitg.ac.in (or through the Coursera-hosted listing); JEE Advanced-eligible candidates get direct admission, others go through a qualifier test.

**MTech (FWRM, Robotics & AI):** Multi-entry/multi-exit structure; GATE explicitly not required; teaching is fully online with recorded videos, live sessions, and discussion hours, mostly delivered by IIT Guwahati faculty; final examinations held at IIT Guwahati at the end of each trimester (an in-person component, despite the otherwise-online delivery).
