## ⚠️ Read this before publishing — active, unresolved regulatory dispute

This is a materially different situation from Amity or Andhra and needs to be handled with extra care:

- In **March 2022**, the UGC issued a public notice stating Annamalai University had been running its Open & Distance Learning (ODL) programmes **without UGC approval since the 2014–15 academic year**, declared post-2014-15 ODL degrees **invalid**, and warned students not to enroll (Careers360 education-news reporting).
- Annamalai University challenged this in the **Madras High Court**; the matter escalated, and related litigation was still active in the **Supreme Court as of February 2026** — the most recent record found (9 Feb 2026, *UGC vs. Annamalai University*, on IndianKanoon) shows the Supreme Court directing the **UGC itself to decide the consequences of annulling degrees found illegal**, indicating the underlying question is **still not finally resolved** as of the most recent information available.
- At the same time, numerous current marketing/aggregator sites (dated through mid-2026) confidently state Annamalai's Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE, formerly DDE) **is** UGC-DEB approved and NAAC A+ accredited, with no mention of the litigation at all.
- A Collegedunia student Q&A directly contradicts the university's own marketing, stating plainly: *"No, Annamalai University Distance Education is not approved by UGC... the institute has filed a case in the High Court as it was previously approved. But there is no final decision yet."*

**Recommendation:** do not publish a simple ✅ "UGC-DEB Approved" badge for Annamalai University the way it's done for Amity or Andhra. This needs a direct, current check against the official UGC-DEB approved-institutions list before publishing anything, and ideally a note that approval has been legally contested. Given the stakes (degree validity for enrolled students), this is the single most important thing to verify before this page goes live.

## About

Annamalai University is a large public state university in Chidambaram, Tamil Nadu, established in 1929. It was among the first Indian universities to offer postgraduate distance-education programs (from 1979, via its Directorate of Distance Education, now renamed the Centre for Distance and Online Education / CDOE). It remains one of the largest distance-education providers in the country by enrollment. However, its distance/online programmes have been the subject of a serious, multi-year regulatory and legal dispute with the UGC over approval status (see flag above) that does not appear to be fully resolved as of the most recent records found.

## Highlights

- One of India's oldest and largest distance-education operations by enrollment (74+ study centres per one source)
- First in India to offer certain postgraduate distance programs (Applied Psychology, Physics, Chemistry, Zoology, Botany, Bioinformatics, Law)

- UGC-DEB approval status: **actively disputed** — see flags; do not present as a simple fact
- Large course catalog (aggregator figures range from 40 to 600+ programs depending on whether on-campus affiliated-college programs are included — see flag)
