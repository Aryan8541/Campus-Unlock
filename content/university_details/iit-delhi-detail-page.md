## ⚠️ Read this before anything else

**IIT Delhi's online offering is structurally different from every other university profiled in this project, in two important ways:**

1. **IITs are not under UGC's jurisdiction, and "UGC-DEB approval" simply doesn't apply to them.** Per the UGC-DEB's own official FAQ page: *"IITs/IIMs/IISc and other INIs [Institutes of National Importance] doesn't come under the purview of UGC."* IIT Delhi is established under the Institutes of Technology Act and operates under its own statutory authority to confer degrees — it doesn't need, and can't get, "UGC-DEB approval" the way Amity, SASTRA, or the other universities in this project do. This isn't a red flag or a gap in IIT Delhi's legitimacy; it's a genuinely different (and arguably higher-authority) legal basis. But it does mean the "UGC-DEB approved" language used elsewhere on this platform isn't the right framing for this page, and shouldn't be applied here even as an "unconfirmed" field — it's simply not applicable.
2. **What IIT Delhi currently offers online is a Postgraduate Diploma, not a full degree.** The only structured, ongoing online academic program found is the **Online PG Diploma Programme** run through CEP-QIP (cepqip.iitd.ac.in). IIT Delhi's actual degree programs — B.Tech, M.Tech, MS, MDes, MA, MSc — are explicitly confirmed as full-time, on-campus, and entrance-exam based (JEE Main for B.Tech; GATE for M.Tech/MS; CEED for MDes). One source explicitly states IIT Delhi "does not offer" a part-time/online M.Tech. A PG Diploma and a Master's degree are not the same credential, and this page should not imply otherwise.

**Recommended framing:** present this page clearly as IIT Delhi's Online PG Diploma offering — a genuine, legitimate, faculty-delivered credential from a top institute — rather than positioning it alongside full UGC-DEB "online degree" listings as if it were an equivalent product.

## About

IIT Delhi, founded in 1961, is one of India's premier institutes of technology and a designated Institute of National Importance. It offers its regular B.Tech, M.Tech, MS, MDes, MA, and MSc degrees exclusively on-campus through national entrance exams. For learners who can't relocate or pause their careers, IIT Delhi runs an **Online PG Diploma Programme** through its Continuing Education Programme (CEP-QIP) division, designed in alignment with NEP 2020 to let working professionals access IIT Delhi faculty and curriculum remotely.

## Highlights

- Delivered directly by IIT Delhi faculty, not outsourced to a third-party ed-tech platform for its core online PG Diploma offering
- Designed in alignment with NEP 2020, per the official CEP-QIP page
- Separately, IIT Delhi also offers 7 short-term online certificate courses (Digital Marketing, Data Science, HR Management, Business Management, Design Thinking & Innovation, Industrial Design, Innovation and Entrepreneurship — per one aggregator)
- Some executive-education-style online programs are also offered in partnership with Emeritus, a third-party ed-tech platform — distinct from CEP-QIP's own directly-run diploma
