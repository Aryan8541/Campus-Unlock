## About

The University of Rajasthan was established on 8 January 1947, originally under the name **University of Rajputana**, and is one of the older state universities in India, conducting teaching and research across 36 departments, 12 centers, and 126 affiliated colleges, with PhD offerings in 37 specializations and MPhil in 36. Its distance-education arm, the **Directorate of Distance Education (DDE)**, was established under Section 2(f) of the UGC Act, 1956, and offers UG, PG, Diploma, Certificate, and even PhD programs in distance mode at what aggregator sources describe as affordable prices.

⚠️ **Headline finding:** The DDE's own official website (ddeuniraj-ac.in), fetched directly for this research pass, displays the specific status line **"Application Submitted to UGC/DEB for Recognition to Offer ODL Programmes"** — phrasing that describes an application *in process*, not a confirmed, granted entitlement. This contrasts with multiple aggregator sources that flatly describe the University of Rajasthan's distance programs as already "UGC-approved" and NAAC A+-validated without qualification. **This is structurally similar to the University of Hyderabad's CDVL case in an earlier batch (official source shows a more conservative, "in progress" status than aggregators claim), though the exact scope and current status of this particular application (which programs, which academic session, whether it has since been approved) could not be determined from the single snapshot of the official page available in this pass.** Recommend treating UGC-DEB entitlement as **unconfirmed and requiring direct verification against the current deb.ugc.ac.in HEI list** before publishing this university as DEB-approved, rather than accepting the aggregator framing at face value.

**Institutional name-confusion note:** Search results for "University of Rajasthan distance education" surfaced at least three genuinely distinct institutions that should not be conflated: (1) **University of Rajasthan** itself (Jaipur, state university, est. 1947 — the subject of this profile), (2) **Central University of Rajasthan (CURAJ)** (Ajmer, a separate central university, est. 2009, with its own distinct Centre for Open Distance Learning), and (3) **Jaipur National University (JNU)** (Jaipur, a private university with its own distinct SODEL distance-education wing) — this is directly analogous to the JNU/Jaipur National University naming confusion flagged for Jawaharlal Nehru University in an earlier batch, but this time involving three institutions rather than two, all genuinely operating in or near Jaipur.
