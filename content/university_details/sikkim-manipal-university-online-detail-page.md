## About

Sikkim Manipal University (SMU) is a private university in Gangtok, Sikkim, established in 1995 through a public-private partnership between the Government of Sikkim and the Manipal Education & Medical Group — one source describes this as India's first such PPP model in higher education, though this specific claim is not independently corroborated. SMU's distance-education wing, founded in 2001, has educated a large number of students over more than two decades and is now delivered online through the "Online Manipal" platform, the shared digital hub for Manipal group institutions. SMU Online offers UG and PG programs in commerce, arts, and computer applications.

## Highlights

## ⚠️ Flags — things to double-check before publishing

1. **MBA fee figures cluster around ₹1,10,000–1,20,000** but don't fully agree — verify directly against smu.onlinemanipal.com before publishing a single number.
2. **Distance-education-arm founding year (2001) is sometimes conflated with the university's overall founding year (1995)** by at least one aggregator (CareerBracket describes "Sikkim Manipal Online University, established in 2001" as if that were the university's founding) — keep these two dates distinct.
3. **Placement package figures vary by scope** (₹4–15 LPA overall vs. ₹5–7 LPA MBA-specific) and one source explicitly flags that SMU does not publish official salary data — treat any package figure as an unofficial estimate, not a confirmed university statistic.
4. **"AI-powered/AI-enabled LMS" claim** appears in two sources but isn't detailed further — treat as a marketing descriptor rather than a verified technical claim.
5. Established year (1995), private-university status, NAAC A+, UGC-DEB approval, and the NIRF 151–200 band ranking are all well-corroborated and consistent — this is one of the more internally consistent profiles in this research batch, alongside Chandigarh University Online.
