## ⚠️ Read this before anything else

IIT Roorkee's online offering sits between the two extremes found elsewhere in this project. Unlike IIT Madras and IIT Kanpur (which run large, well-established full online degree programs), and unlike IIT Kharagpur (where no full online degree could be confirmed at all), **IIT Roorkee has at least one confirmed genuine online degree** — an Online M.Tech in Microelectronics and Very-Large Scale Integration (VLSI), launched in the 2021-22 academic year under the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering — but it appears narrower in scope (one specific specialization, not a broad multi-track catalog) than IIT Madras's or IIT Kanpur's offerings. Beyond that, IIT Roorkee runs an Executive MBA and a range of shorter PG Certificate programs (5G/IoT, Supply Chain with AI, Data Science & ML, VLSI Design, ML for Finance) through its E&ICT Academy — these are certificate-level, not degree-level, and shouldn't be conflated with the VLSI M.Tech.

**Same jurisdictional point as every other IIT in this project:** UGC-DEB approval doesn't apply here — IIT Roorkee, as an Institute of National Importance, sits outside UGC's purview, and confers its own degrees under its own statutory authority.

## About

IIT Roorkee traces its roots to 1847, making it one of the oldest technical institutes in Asia, with its main campus in Roorkee plus additional campuses in Saharanpur and Greater Noida. Alongside its extensive on-campus catalog (14+ undergraduate programs, 24+ postgraduate programs), IIT Roorkee introduced an Online M.Tech program in Microelectronics and VLSI in 2021-22 under its Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering. It also runs an Executive MBA for working professionals, and a set of shorter PG Certificate programs through its E&ICT (Electronics & ICT) Academy.

## Highlights

- Online M.Tech in Microelectronics and VLSI — a genuine degree-level online program, introduced under NEP 2020's philosophy of blending practical and professional learning
- Executive MBA available for working professionals (distinct from the full-time on-campus MBA, which requires CAT + Personal Interview)
- E&ICT Academy PG Certificate programs across 5G/IoT, Strategic Supply Chain with AI, Data Science & Machine Learning, VLSI Design, and Machine Learning for Finance
- 40,000+ global alumni network cited by one aggregator
