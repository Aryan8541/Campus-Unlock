## About

Dr. B.R. Ambedkar Open University (BRAOU), originally established in **1982** as **Andhra Pradesh Open University** — the first open university in India — was renamed in 1991 and is headquartered in Jubilee Hills, Hyderabad. As a dedicated open university (not a distance wing bolted onto a conventional university, unlike most institutions profiled earlier in this research series), BRAOU's entire mission centers on flexible, accessible distance education, delivered through a network of **180 Learner Support Centres, including 23 Regional Coordination Centres (RCCs)**, spanning Telangana and Andhra Pradesh. Courses are structured as foundation courses, core courses, and application-oriented courses, delivered through both print and electronic modes, and taught in both **English and Telugu**.
