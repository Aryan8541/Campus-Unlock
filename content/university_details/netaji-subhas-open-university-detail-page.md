## About

Netaji Subhas Open University (NSOU) is a state open university headquartered in Kolkata, established on 25 November 1997 — named to commemorate the birth centenary of Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose — making it the 10th open university in India. NSOU offers open and distance learning (ODL) programs across Arts, Science, Commerce, Education, Management, and Professional/Vocational Studies through a network of 100+ study centres across West Bengal, and has more recently added online-mode delivery alongside its traditional ODL programs.

## Highlights

- UGC-DEB approved — confirmed on the official wbnsou.ac.in admission portal, citing specific UGC-DEB approval file numbers
- NAAC A grade accredited
- NIRF #2 among Open Universities in India (2024 ranking), behind only IGNOU
- 47 programmes across Humanities, Social Sciences, Sciences, Education, Professional Studies, and Vocational Studies
- UGC 12B status
- Also recognized by AICTE and RCI (Rehabilitation Council of India) for specific professional programs (e.g., B.Ed in Special Education)

## Admission Process

1. Apply via the official portal (wbnsou.ac.in)
2. Submit ABC ID and DEB ID — mandatory for all applicants per current UGC-DEB requirements for ODL-mode enrollment
3. Admission is purely merit-based for most UG/PG/Diploma/BLIS programs (no entrance exam); PhD, B.Ed, and M.Ed admissions are entrance-test based instead
4. Document upload and application fee payment (₹500, per one source)
5. Upload payment confirmation and download the provisional enrollment certificate and Study Learning Material (SLM) token

## ⚠️ Flags — things to double-check before publishing

1. **Enrollment figures conflict dramatically across sources**: one source cites 4.75 lakh learners, another cites 2.5 lakh, and Wikipedia's infobox states only 41,587 total students (2025) — a roughly 10x spread between the lowest and highest figures. This is a much larger discrepancy than the usual single-outlier pattern seen elsewhere and is worth resolving directly with NSOU before publishing any enrollment figure, since it's unclear whether these numbers are measuring the same thing (e.g., cumulative historical enrollment vs. currently active students).
2. **One low-quality source claimed NSOU "started functioning from July 1988"** — this conflicts with the well-corroborated 1997 establishment date found everywhere else (including Wikipedia and the official domain) and looks like an error, possibly confusion with a different distance-education body.
3. **NIRF rank has a minor conflict**: #2 (most sources) vs. #3 (one aggregator) for the Open University category.
4. **No placement data exists anywhere** — this isn't a case of conflicting figures to resolve, but a genuine absence; decide deliberately how (or whether) to represent placement fields for a university where this concept may not really apply in the way it does for professional-degree-focused universities.
5. Everything else (UGC-DEB approval, NAAC A grade, 1997 establishment, official wbnsou.ac.in domain) is well-corroborated across official and independent sources — no changes needed there.
