## 1. Hero

- **Display name:** Bharathidasan University (BDU)
- **Short name / slug:** `bharathidasan-university`
- **Type:** State Government University (public) — explicitly **not** private, confirmed via official CDOE FAQ
- **City / State:** Tiruchirappalli (Palkalaiperur campus), Tamil Nadu
- **Established:** February 1982; Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE, also referred to as CDE) founded in 1992
- **Modes offered:** Both Distance (ODL) and Online, run through the same Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE)
- **One-line positioning:** A Tamil Nadu state university with a long-running, large-scale distance-education wing (since 1992) that has more recently added a smaller online-mode catalogue under UGC-DEB entitlement, named after the Tamil poet Bharathidasan.

## 2. About

Bharathidasan University was established in February 1982 and is a **state government university**, not a private or deemed institution — this is explicitly confirmed on the CDOE's own FAQ page and is a meaningful differentiator from MAHER and CHRIST in this batch (both deemed/private). Its Centre for Distance and Online Education was founded in 1992 to serve students who could not access regular on-campus education, and now runs both distance-mode (self-study material + Personal Contact Programs) and online-mode (fully remote, UGC-DEB-entitled) tracks under one administrative umbrella.

## 3. Highlights

- NAAC accreditation: **A+** (one source states A++ and a 3.61 score — **conflicting grade notation across sources, needs reconciliation**; A+ appears more frequently across sources and is likely correct)

## 11. Learning Methodology

- Distance mode: self-study materials, e-content/question-bank portal, PCP attendance requirement for select programs (2–3 weeks), weekend contact classes
- Online mode: combination of theory and practical/lab learning, supported by PCP centres and LSCs, per aggregator description — the online mode appears less "fully asynchronous" than a typical pure-online program, since it borrows some of the distance-mode's PCP infrastructure. This is a useful distinguishing note for the CampusUnlock methodology field ODL vs. Online, since BDU's "online" track isn't a clean pure-online build the way some other universities' online wings are.
- Grievance redressal via a dedicated web portal (per official-adjacent source)

## 12. FAQs (draft, partly sourced from official CDOE FAQ)

- **Is Bharathidasan University private?** No — confirmed by the university's own FAQ: it is a State Government University.
- **Are CDOE programs UGC recognized?** Yes, per the official FAQ, with the caveat that students should verify current-session DEB approval status themselves via the link provided by the university.
- **Is home-based study always allowed?** Mostly yes, but some programs require attendance at BDU or designated PCP centres for practicals, labs, or contact sessions of about 2–3 weeks, per the official FAQ.
- **Does BDU use franchise/study-centre partners?** No — the official FAQ explicitly states BDU does not operate through franchise-mode admissions, which is a meaningful trust signal worth surfacing on the comparison platform given how common franchise-model complaints are in Indian distance education.
